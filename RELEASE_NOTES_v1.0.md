# Cabi Platform - Release Notes v1.0.0

**Date:** 2024-06-07
**Version:** 1.0.0 (MVP)
**Status:** Stable Release

## 🚀 Release Highlights

We are proud to announce the official release of **Cabi v1.0.0**. This milestone marks the completion of our Minimum Viable Product (MVP), delivering a fully functional ride-hailing ecosystem. This release introduces comprehensive operational tools, financial automation, safety features, and growth engines.

### Key Modules Delivered:
*   **Advanced Analytics**: Real-time visibility into operations and finances.
*   **Driver Payouts**: Automated weekly earnings processing.
*   **Passenger Loyalty**: Gamified rewards system for retention.
*   **Driver Safety**: Enhanced security tools for drivers.
*   **Marketing Engine**: Promo codes and targeted push notifications.
*   **Corporate Accounts**: B2B management for business travel.

---

## 🛠️ New Features

### 📊 Admin Portal (Operations & Finance)
*   **Real-time Heatmap**: Visualize demand and active trips live on an interactive map.
*   **Financial Dashboard**: Track total revenue, commissions, and SACCO savings with 30-day trend analysis.
*   **Driver Performance**: Monitor driver metrics including acceptance rates, ratings, and total trips.
*   **Payout Processing**: Review pending driver balances and trigger payouts manually or via schedule.
*   **Marketing Campaigns**: Create promo codes and send targeted push notifications to specific user segments.
*   **Corporate Management**: Register B2B accounts, manage employees, and view corporate spending reports.
*   **Exportable Reports**: Download CSV reports for Financials, Driver Performance, and Trip Logs.

### 🚗 Driver App
*   **Earnings Dashboard**: View daily and weekly earnings summaries with visual charts.
*   **Payout Methods**: Manage M-Pesa and Bank Account details for receiving payments.
*   **Safety Toolkit**: Quick access to "Share Trip Details" and SOS emergency assistance.
*   **Passenger Verification**: "ID Verified" badge displayed on incoming ride requests for enhanced safety.

### 📱 Passenger App
*   **Loyalty Program**: Earn points on every ride, view history, and redeem points for ride discounts.
*   **Promotions**: Apply promo codes from the wallet section to receive fare discounts.
*   **Corporate Payment**: Select "Corporate Account" as a payment method for business rides.
*   **Spending Summary**: View total spending summary on the trip history screen.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes & Improvements
