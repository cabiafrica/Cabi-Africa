* **4.1. Mandatory Onboarding Declaration:** During onboarding and profile maintenance, the Driver has an **affirmative legal obligation to declare whether their Vehicle is fitted with an in-cabin camera or dashcam system**, and must upload proof of installation and interior warning decals.

* **4.3. Automated Rolling Overwrite:** In-cabin camera footage must operate on an automated rolling loop overwriting every 48 to 72 hours, except where an active police or safety investigation is underway.
