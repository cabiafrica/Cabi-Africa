1. POLICY STATEMENT, STATUTORY MANDATE & GUIDING PRINCIPLES

CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED (BRS Registration Number PVT-OD169L8L, Postal Address: P.O. Box 64844 - 00620, Nairobi, Kenya) is dedicated to maintaining a safe, transparent, and equitable digital transport ecosystem across the Republic of Kenya.

Pursuant to Regulation 14 of the National Transport and Safety Authority (Operation of Transport Network Companies) Regulations, Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022, and in strict adherence to the constitutional guarantees of fair administrative action enshrined under Article 47 of the Constitution of Kenya and the Fair Administrative Action Act (No. 4 of 2015):

  1. No Arbitrary Deactivation: No driver partner or vehicle owner shall be arbitrarily, summarily, or unfairly deactivated, suspended, or barred from the Cabi Africa platform without adherence to documented due process.
  2. Rules of Natural Justice: Every disciplinary or administrative decision must strictly uphold the rules of natural justice, including prompt written notification of allegations, the right to inspect evidence, a meaningful opportunity to be heard, and reasoned written determinations.
  3. Objective Evidentiary Standards: Account actions shall be founded strictly on objective, verifiable data (GPS telematics, in-cabin surveillance footage where installed, and communication logs) rather than uncorroborated verbal allegations.

2. CATEGORIZATION OF ACCOUNT ACTIONS & OFFENSES

Account disciplinary actions are classified into three distinct, structured categories:

Category A: Immediate Precautionary Suspension (Zero-Tolerance Safety Triggers)

Enacted instantly to protect public safety while an urgent, formal investigation is conducted:

  • Credible reports of sexual misconduct, assault, physical violence, or brandishing dangerous weapons;
  • Operating a vehicle under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or psychotropic substances (DUI);
  • Suspected commission of criminal offenses, kidnapping, vehicle carjacking, or extortion;
  • Unauthorized subletting, leasing, or sharing of verified driver account credentials with an unregistered third party;
  • Unlawful Dashcam Leaks (ODPC Section 72): Extracting, copying, downloading, or leaking in-cabin audio or video surveillance footage of passengers to social media platforms (TikTok, Twitter, WhatsApp, Facebook) in direct violation of Section 72 of the Data Protection Act (2019);
  • Severe roadworthiness defects posing imminent structural or mechanical danger (e.g., failed braking systems, bald tires).

Category B: Corrective Warnings, Temporary Holds & Account Reset (Operational Infractions)

Enacted for non-safety operational or quality infractions:

  • Aggregate driver rating falling below the published regional benchmark (4.50 out of 5.00 stars evaluated over 100 consecutive completed journeys), following prior corrective guidance;
  • Unprovoked cancellation rates after trip acceptance exceeding the platform threshold (> 25% without operational or safety justification);
  • Attempting to demand off-app cash fares above the in-app upfront estimate or refusing to accept digital payments;
  • Expiry of statutory credentials (Driving License, NTSA PSV Badge, Police Clearance Certificate of Good Conduct, Vehicle Inspection Certificate, or Commercial PSV Insurance).

Account Restoration & Reset Mechanism for Category B:

Category B operational holds do not trigger permanent account termination. A driver placed on Category B hold may immediately reset and restore active dispatch status by:

  1. Administrative Reset Surcharge: Settling a nominal administrative reinstatement fee (KES 500 to KES 1,000) via mobile money or in-app wallet balance deduction; OR
  2. In-App Customer Care Refresher Module: Completing a mandatory 15-minute digital micro-module on passenger etiquette and professional standards; OR
  3. Document Renewal Verification: Uploading verified, authentic renewed statutory certificates where the hold was triggered by document expiry.

Once the corrective requirement is satisfied, the dispatch system automatically restores full account functionality.

Category C: Immediate Permanent Termination (Gross Misconduct & Fraud)

Enacted following conclusive verification of:

  • Submission of forged, falsified, or stolen documents (counterfeit DCI Good Conduct Certificate, forged NTSA badge, fraudulent vehicle logbook);
  • Final conviction in a competent court of law for a criminal felony, dangerous driving causing death, or sexual violence;
  • Intentional platform cyber-sabotage, GPS spoofing, app cloning, or collusive fare manipulation scams.

Procedural Safeguards:

  1. Written Notice of Allegation: Within four (4) hours of any precautionary hold or disciplinary action, Cabi shall issue an official digital notice to the Driver Partner specifying:
  • The exact contractual clause or policy breached;
  • The date, approximate time, and nature of the incident (with passenger personal identity masked to protect privacy under the Data Protection Act);
  • The temporary or permanent nature of the administrative hold.
  1. Right to Respond and Submit Evidence: The Driver Partner shall have seventy-two (72) hours to submit their written statement, dashcam video footage, GPS tracking records, witness contacts, or explanations via the Driver App or in person at the Walk-in Dispute Desk.
  2. The 48-Hour Investigatory Hold Hard Cap: An investigatory precautionary freeze shall not exceed forty-eight (48) hours without a formal interim status communication from the Safety Desk. If an investigation requires extended liaison with police or forensic inspection of dashcam archives, the driver partner must be provided with an interim status report, preventing indefinite lockout.
  3. Defense Against Malicious Reports & Competitor Sabotage: Where a passenger complaint is determined to be bad-faith, retaliatory, an attempt to evade fare payment, or orchestrated by competitor agents, the driver partner shall be immediately exonerated, all related cancellation metrics and 1-star ratings expunged, and the fraudulent complainant permanently blacklisted.

4. WALLET BALANCE RING-FENCING & 7-DAY PAYOUT GUARANTEE

CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED upholds the fundamental legal principle that disciplinary action shall not constitute financial confiscation or conversion of earned compensation:

  1. No Forfeiture of Honest Income: Account deactivation or termination never results in the forfeiture, seizure, or cancellation of earned, undisputed trip earnings.
  2. Mandatory Payout SLA: All legitimate net balances remaining in the driver partner's wallet upon account termination shall be processed and disbursed via Mobile Money or registered bank account within seven (7) business days following deactivation.
  3. Permissible Deductions: Deductions from final wallet balances are strictly limited to substantiated, verified passenger fare debts, unremitted statutory tolls, or proven fraud losses.

5. THE VEHICLE-DRIVER DECOUPLING PROTOCOL (FLEET OWNER ASSET SHIELD)

To protect third-party vehicle owners, hire-purchase financiers, and SACCO investors from bearing collateral damage for individual driver misconduct:

  1. Strict Decoupling of Asset from Driver: The administrative suspension or permanent deactivation of an individual driver partner shall not taint, freeze, or blacklist the associated motor vehicle.
  2. Owner Notification: The registered vehicle owner or fleet manager shall be formally notified within twelve (12) hours of any driver deactivation affecting their enlisted vehicle.
  3. Immediate Vehicle Reassignment Right: The vehicle owner maintains the automatic statutory right to immediately disassociate the deactivated driver on the Fleet Portal and assign the compliant vehicle to another verified, accredited Cabi driver partner without administrative delays or inspection re-fees.

6. INDEPENDENT APPEALS COMMITTEE (IAC)

To guarantee impartial review, CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED has established the Independent Appeals Committee (IAC):

6.1. Composition of the Committee:

  1. Chairperson: An external certified dispute mediator or practicing Advocate of the High Court of Kenya;
  2. Member: An independent driver representative nominated by registered Driver Welfare Associations or the Cabi Driver Advisory Council;
  3. Member: Cabi Head of Safety & Operations (or designated compliance officer recused if previously involved in the primary investigation).

6.2. Appeals Workflow:

  • Any driver partner or vehicle owner subject to a Category A or Category C deactivation may lodge a formal appeal within fourteen (14) calendar days of the decision via the in-app Appeal Form, by email to cabifastafrica@gmail.com, or by physical delivery to the Head Office.
  • The IAC convenes weekly to hear appeals.
  • The appellant has the legal right to attend the hearing (in-person or virtually), be accompanied by a legal representative or peer advocate, inspect evidence, and present witnesses.
  • The IAC shall render a written, reasoned determination within seven (7) business days of the hearing.

7. REPUTATIONAL EXPUNGEMENT & RATING REHABILITATION

Where a driver partner is exonerated by the Compliance Review Team or the Independent Appeals Committee:

  1. Immediate Account Restoration: Active dispatch capability is restored within two (2) hours of the ruling.
  2. Algorithmic Record Cleansing: The platform automatically deletes the disputed 1-star rating, expunges the incident from driver cancellation metrics, and mathematically recalculates the driver's aggregate rating to restore their historical benchmark standing.
  3. Priority Dispatch Recovery: The driver partner is assigned regular algorithmic dispatch priority without latent algorithmic penalization.

8. STATUTORY OBLIGATIONS & RECORD RETENTION

  • NTSA Inspection Compliance: In accordance with Regulation 14 of Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022, a quarterly audit summary of all suspensions, deactivations, and appeal outcomes shall be submitted to the NTSA Director of Road Safety.
  • Archival Mandate: All deactivation notices, written submissions, dashcam logs, and IAC rulings shall be securely retained for a minimum statutory period of five (5) years.
