### RECITALS:
**WHEREAS:**
A. The Company operates a technology-enabled passenger transportation and delivery network platform providing digital dispatch aggregation, live route tracking, driver identity verification, passenger identity authentication, and payment facilitation systems (the **"Cabi Platform"**) in compliance with the **National Transport and Safety Authority Act (No. 33 of 2012)** and the **National Transport and Safety Authority (Transport Network Companies, Owners, Drivers and Passengers) Regulations, 2022**.

### NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

### 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
Unless the context otherwise requires:
1.1. **“Approved Driver”** means an individual professional driver or rider independently vetted, screened, and approved in writing by the Company to operate the Vehicle on the Cabi Platform.  
1.2. **“Vehicle”** means the motor car, van, or commercial motorcycle described in Schedule 1, owned by or lawfully under the control of the Vehicle Owner and approved by the Company for commercial passenger or courier services.  
1.3. **“Services”** means the provision of passenger transportation, cab, ride-hailing, transfer, or courier delivery services arranged through the Cabi Platform.  
1.4. **“Gross Revenue”** means all gross fares charged and received in respect of completed trips before statutory deductions.  
1.5. **“Applicable Law”** means the laws and regulations in force in Kenya, including the NTSA TNC Regulations (2022), the Traffic Act (Cap 403), the Insurance Act, the Data Protection Act (2019), and tax legislation.

---

### 2. ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE OF SERVICES
2.1. The Company engages the Vehicle Owner, and the Vehicle Owner accepts the engagement, to make the Vehicle available together with a suitably qualified and Approved Driver for the provision of Services through the Cabi Platform.  
2.2. The Vehicle Owner shall ensure that the Vehicle and the Approved Driver are available for accepted bookings and provide Services safely, diligently, professionally, and in strict adherence to this Agreement.  
2.3. The Vehicle shall be used only for lawful transportation assignments authorized through the Cabi Platform, unless the Company grants prior written consent to other corporate usage.  
2.4. The Vehicle Owner shall not, and shall ensure that the Approved Driver does not, represent that either of them has authority to enter into contracts, incur liabilities, or bind the Company without prior written authorization.  
2.5. The Company does not guarantee any minimum number of bookings, trip volumes, hours of work, or revenue targets.

---

### 3. NATURE OF RELATIONSHIP & TRIPARTITE SEPARATION
3.1. **Independent Service Provider:** The Vehicle Owner contracts with the Company as an independent service provider and not as an employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer.  
3.2. **Approved Driver Relationship:** The Vehicle Owner acknowledges that where a third-party driver is engaged to operate the Vehicle:
* The Approved Driver is recruited, contracted, and engaged directly by the Vehicle Owner.
* The Vehicle Owner is solely responsible, as between the Parties, for all private contractual terms, hiring targets, vehicle rental models, remuneration, working hours, and employment compliance under the Employment Act, 2007.  
3.3. **Driver-App Operational Relationship:** Cabi’s relationship with the Approved Driver is strictly an operational software platform user relationship governed by the *Driver Services Agreement*.

---

### 4. VEHICLE ENLISTMENT, ROADWORTHINESS & SECONDARY INSPECTIONS
4.1. **Proof of Ownership / Authority:** The Vehicle Owner warrants that it is the lawful registered proprietor of the Vehicle or holds a notarized Commercial Lease Agreement / Power of Attorney granting full authority to deploy the Vehicle for commercial digital hailing. Proof of ownership (Logbook Form E) must be submitted before onboarding.  
4.2. **Statutory Inspection & Secondary Roadworthiness Layer:**
* The Vehicle must hold an active, valid **NTSA Annual Motor Vehicle Inspection Certificate** certifying roadworthiness.
* To ensure superior public safety, the Vehicle Owner covenants to submit the Vehicle for secondary mechanical and safety certification through an accredited automotive service and roadworthiness inspection center.  
4.3. **Motorcycle Standards (where applicable):** Commercial motorcycles must maintain dual certified helmets, two reflective branded jackets, dual rear mirrors, clean headlights/indicators, and IRA-approved commercial motorcycle insurance.  
4.4. **In-Cabin Dashcam & Safety Systems:**
* The Vehicle Owner is encouraged to equip the Vehicle with an approved dual-channel safety dashcam (recording road ahead and vehicle cabin).
* Dashcams must feature tamper-evident storage and automatic rolling 48-to-72-hour loop overwriting.
* The Vehicle Owner shall ensure a physical notification decal is prominently displayed inside the passenger cabin notifying occupants of the camera system in compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2019.  
4.5. **Routine Servicing & Repairs:** The Vehicle Owner shall be solely responsible for routine servicing, tires, brake maintenance, engine repair, and mechanical integrity.  
4.6. **Defect Notification:** The Vehicle Owner shall promptly notify the Company of any mechanical breakdown, structural defect, brake failure, or accident affecting the Vehicle’s roadworthiness.  
4.7. **No Ownership Acquired by Company:** The Company does not acquire any legal, equitable, or beneficial ownership interest in the Vehicle by reason of this Agreement.

---

### 5. DRIVER QUALIFICATIONS, SECONDARY TRUST & SUBSTITUTION
5.1. Before an Approved Driver operates the Vehicle, the Vehicle Owner shall ensure the driver submits:
a. National Identity Card or valid Passport (confirming age 24+ for passenger cars);  
b. Valid Kenyan Smart Driving License endorsed for **Class D1** (or **Class A2/A3** for motorcycles);  
c. Valid NTSA PSV Driver Badge (where applicable);  
d. Valid Police Clearance Certificate (Certificate of Good Conduct) from the DCI issued within the preceding six (6) months;  
e. Secondary identity and background vetting clearance through an accredited third-party background and trust verification agency.  
5.2. **Replacement or Substitute Drivers:** The Vehicle Owner shall strictly notify the Company before substituting any driver. Under no circumstances shall an unvetted or unapproved driver operate an enlisted Vehicle on the Platform. Permitting an unverified driver to drive results in immediate vehicle deactivation.

---

### 6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE VEHICLE OWNER
The Vehicle Owner shall:
6.1. Maintain the Vehicle in a pristine, safe, clean, and mechanically roadworthy condition at all times.  
6.2. Ensure the Approved Driver observes all traffic laws, speed limits, passenger-safety rules, and Company operational codes.  
6.3. Ensure the Approved Driver never drives while intoxicated, fatigued, or under the influence of drugs.  
6.4. Promptly report accidents, passenger safety incidents, vehicle impoundments, or police investigations to the Company within two (2) hours of occurrence.  
6.5. Ensure the Approved Driver complies with passenger personal data confidentiality under the Data Protection Act, 2019, including the strict prohibition against copying or publishing camera recordings.  
6.6. Cooperate fully with the Company, National Police Service, and NTSA in any safety or fraud investigation.

---

### 7. OBLIGATIONS OF THE COMPANY
The Company shall:
7.1. Provide the Vehicle Owner access to the *Cabi Fleet Management Portal* displaying real-time vehicle mileage, completed trips, active driver assignments, and document expiry alerts.  
7.2. Maintain a responsive 24/7 Safety and Incident Response Center reachable at **+254 758 777 222**.  
7.3. Ensure platform dispatch algorithms disallow trips to vehicles whose statutory inspection, insurance, or driver licenses have expired.  
7.4. Process all personal and corporate records in compliance with Kenyan data privacy laws.

---

### 8. FINANCIAL MODEL: EXCLUSIVE DRIVER-APP DISBURSEMENTS
8.1. **Direct Driver-App Settlement:** In accordance with Cabi's platform architecture:
* All digital trip disbursements, fares, and customer payments are credited and settled directly to the operating **Approved Driver’s in-app wallet / mobile money account**.
* Cabi deducts only its statutory platform commission of eighteen percent (18%) from the gross fare in strict compliance with NTSA regulations.  
8.2. **Zero Platform Involvement in Owner-Driver Rentals:**
* Cabi is **not a party to, nor financially responsible for**, collecting, deducting, or enforcing daily vehicle hire rates, rental targets, lease fees, or private revenue-sharing agreements between the Vehicle Owner and the Approved Driver.
* All commercial terms regarding car rental targets, fuel allocations, servicing costs, and hire-purchase terms are private matters agreed independently between the Vehicle Owner and the Driver.  
8.3. **Tax Compliance:** The Vehicle Owner is responsible for its own commercial income tax, corporate tax, or asset depreciation obligations under Kenyan tax law.

---

### 9. FUEL, MAINTENANCE AND OPERATING EXPENSES
9.1. The Vehicle Owner shall bear all costs of vehicle ownership, licensing, routine maintenance, parts replacement, tires, and mechanical repairs.  
9.2. The Vehicle Owner shall not incur any expenditure in the Company’s name or attempt to bind the Company to third-party mechanical debts.

---

### 10. INSURANCE AND ACCIDENTS
10.1. **Comprehensive Commercial PSV Cover:** The Vehicle Owner shall maintain uninterrupted **Commercial PSV (Digital Hailing / Chauffeur-Driven) Comprehensive Insurance** covering Passenger Legal Liability (PLL), third-party property damage, and bodily injury.  
10.2. **Annual Evidence of Renewal:** The Vehicle Owner shall upload renewed insurance certificates thirty (30) days prior to expiration. The vehicle is automatically suspended from dispatch upon insurance lapse.  
10.3. **Accident Procedure:** In the event of an accident, the Vehicle Owner shall ensure the Approved Driver stops, renders medical aid, contacts the police, notifies Cabi's 24/7 line (**+254 758 777 222**), and notifies the underwriter within twenty-four (24) hours.

---

### 11. PASSENGER SAFETY, LOST PROPERTY & COMPLAINTS
11.1. The Vehicle Owner shall mandate that the Approved Driver prioritizes passenger comfort, road courtesy, and safety at all times.  
11.2. **Lost Property Protocol:** The Vehicle Owner shall ensure that any lost item left in the Vehicle is promptly returned via passenger-arranged delivery or surrendered to the Cabi Head Office (Ridgeways Commercial Centre, Nairobi) or nearest police station within twenty-four (24) hours, to be held under the 90-day custody rules of the Disposal of Uncollected Goods Act.

---

### 12. PROHIBITED CONDUCT AND MATERIAL BREACH
The following constitute material breaches resulting in immediate platform termination:
12.1. Deploying an unapproved, unvetted, or substitute driver without prior written Company authorization;  
12.2. Permitting operation of an unroadworthy, uninspected, or uninsured Vehicle;  
12.3. Submitting falsified logbooks, forged NTSA inspection stickers, or counterfeit insurance policies;  
12.4. Tampering with vehicle speed governors, odometers, or platform GPS telemetry;  
12.5. Unauthorized commercial exploitation of Cabi branding, passenger recordings, or customer personal data.

---

### 13. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY
13.1. Each Party is responsible for its own lawful acts and omissions.  
13.2. The Vehicle Owner shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company, its directors, and staff against any third-party claims, lawsuits, or penalties arising from:
* Mechanical failure or unroadworthiness of the Vehicle;
* The Vehicle Owner’s private employment disputes with the Approved Driver;
* Lapse or invalidity of the vehicle’s commercial insurance policy;
* Unlawful distribution of passenger audio/video recordings in breach of privacy laws.

---

### 14. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
In compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2019, the Vehicle Owner shall maintain strict confidentiality over all customer records, telemetry data, and platform software, and shall never use passenger information for personal solicitation.

---

### 15. TERM AND TERMINATION
15.1. This Agreement commences on the execution date and continues for one (1) year, automatically renewing upon annual submission of valid NTSA inspection and insurance certificates.  
15.2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving **fourteen (14) days’ written notice**.  
15.3. The Company may immediately remove a Vehicle from operational dispatch where a safety hazard, unapproved driver deployment, or legal compliance breach is detected.
