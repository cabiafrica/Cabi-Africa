### WELCOME TO THE CABI COMMUNITY
Cabi Africa connects commuters, professional driver partners, and vehicle fleet operators across Kenya through safe, dignified, and legally compliant mobility technology. To preserve an atmosphere of mutual dignity and physical security, every individual accessing or utilizing the Cabi platform agrees to adhere to this Community Code of Conduct and Safety Charter.

---

### 1. STANDARDS FOR DRIVER PARTNERS

#### 1.1. Road Safety & Responsible Driving
* **Zero Alcohol & Drug Tolerance:** Drivers must never log onto the platform or operate a vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, prescription medication affecting reflexes, or *miraa/muguka*. Zero blood alcohol content (0.00%) is strictly enforced.
* **Traffic Law Compliance:** Obey all national speed limits, lane disciplines, and traffic signals under the Traffic Act (Cap 403). Reckless driving, pavement overlapping, and sidewalk driving are strictly forbidden.
* **Distraction-Free Cab:** Mobile phones must remain securely mounted on an approved dashboard mount. Never text, browse social feeds, or handle a phone while driving.
* **Seatbelt & Helmet Safety:** Drivers must ensure that all occupants wear seatbelts before moving. On motorcycle dispatches, drivers must provide a clean, certified crash helmet and high-visibility reflector vest.
* **Fatigue Management:** A maximum driving cap of 12 cumulative hours per 24-hour cycle is enforced, followed by a mandatory 6-hour offline rest break.

#### 1.2. Professional Courtesy & Non-Discrimination
* **Equal Opportunity Service:** Provide professional service to all riders without discrimination based on ethnicity, gender, religion, race, physical disability, sexual orientation, or age.
* **Vulnerable Passengers:** Assist passengers with disabilities, elderly riders, and expectant mothers with patience and care. Wheelchairs, guide dogs, and mobility aids must be accommodated at zero additional charge.
* **Respect for Boundaries:** Keep conversation respectful and professional. Do not ask prying personal questions regarding marital status, dating life, or finances. Unsolicited physical contact or inappropriate remarks result in immediate deactivation and police reporting.
* **Post-Trip Confidentiality:** Never contact, message, or visit a rider after the trip is concluded. Customer contact details are strictly confidential under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

#### 1.3. In-Cabin Camera & Privacy Integrity
* **Dashcam Notice:** Drivers operating approved dashcams must affix the visible in-cabin notification decal.
* **Social Media Ban:** Under Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, dashcam footage may never be downloaded, extracted, or shared on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, or WhatsApp. Leaking rider footage leads to lifetime blacklisting and criminal prosecution.

### 2. STANDARDS FOR PASSENGERS & COMMUTERS

#### 2.1. Account Authenticity & Profile Avatars
* **Real Identity:** Passengers must register with their true legal name and upload a clear, authentic facial portrait avatar. Pseudonyms and placeholder avatars are prohibited.
* **Driver Visual Authentication:** Drivers hold the safety right to match the commuter’s face against their in-app profile avatar before unlocking vehicle doors.
* **Verification Checks:** Passengers must comply with periodic security verification checks (SMS OTP, facial selfie confirmation) during night-time hours or when booking from designated high-risk zones.

#### 2.2. Civil Respect & Road Safety Cooperation
* **Dignified Conduct:** Treat drivers with courtesy and respect. Verbal harassment, profanity, threats, slamming doors, or damaging vehicle property will result in account suspension and repair surcharges.
* **No Unlawful Driving Demands:** Never pressure or bribe a driver to break traffic rules, exceed speed limits, overlap, or make illegal turns.
* **Strict Capacity Limits (No Overloading):** Under Section 100 of the Traffic Act, saloon cars carry a maximum of 4 passengers. Do not attempt to overload vehicles or place unrestrained children on adult laps.
* **No Illegal Stops (County Clamping Shield):** Passengers must not demand pick-up or drop-off in designated red curbings, yellow lines, or bus stages. Passengers assume full financial liability for any county clamping or towing fines caused by forced illegal stops.

#### 2.3. Cleanliness, Food & Intoxicant Policy
* **No Eating or Open Beverages:** Eating meals, snacks, or drinking open beverages inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited to maintain cabin cleanliness. Only factory-sealed bottled water is allowed.
* **No Smoking, Vaping, Alcohol, or Miraa:** Using tobacco, e-cigarettes (vaping), chewing *miraa/muguka*, or drinking alcohol inside the vehicle is strictly forbidden and warrants immediate trip termination.
* **Cleaning Fee:** Vehicle soiling (vomiting, drink spills, stains, debris) incurs a mandatory cleaning fee between KES 1,500 and KES 3,500 charged directly to the passenger to reimburse professional vehicle detailing.

#### 2.4. Luggage Guidelines
* Standard personal luggage, suitcases, and shopping bags that fit safely in the boot are welcome.
* Transporting hazardous chemicals, heavy construction goods, gas cylinders, raw fish/meat, livestock, or commercial cargo in passenger vehicles is strictly prohibited.

### 3. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE OFFENSES

The following conduct warrants instant, permanent account deactivation and criminal referral to the National Police Service:
1. **Assault & Violence:** Physical battery, fighting, threatening behavior, or brandishing weapons.
2. **Sexual Misconduct:** Uninvited touching, sexual remarks, indecent exposure, or solicitation.
3. **Off-App Fare Poaching:** Soliciting or agreeing to cancel active app dispatches for off-app cash deals. Off-app rides forfeit all passenger safety insurance and platform GPS tracking.
4. **Fare Evasion & M-Pesa Reversal Fraud:** Refusing to settle fares or maliciously reversing mobile money payments after completed rides (Section 316A, Penal Code).
5. **Cyber Extortion & Review Blackmail:** Threatening bad ratings or social media smear campaigns to extort free rides or cash payments (Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018).
6. **Privacy Violations & Doxxing:** Disclosing private phone numbers, trip details, or passenger photos publicly.

* Minor infractions follow progressive coaching, while serious safety breaches trigger immediate suspension.
* All parties maintain the statutory right to appeal deactivations within 14 calendar days before the Cabi Independent Appeals Committee.
