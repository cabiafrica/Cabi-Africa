## 5. BOOKING, VEHICLE CAPACITY, LUGGAGE LIMITS AND DISPATCH RULES

### 5.1 Booking Process and Driver Identification
A passenger requests a ride by inputting designated pickup and drop-off coordinates, selecting a vehicle tier, and reviewing the upfront fare estimate. Upon dispatch, the app transmits the driver's full name, profile photo, aggregate star rating, vehicle make, model, color, and NTSA registration number. **The passenger has an affirmative personal duty to verify the vehicle's registration number plate and driver's face before boarding.**

### 5.2 Strict Passenger Seating Capacity Limits (Section 100 Traffic Act Shield)
1. **Absolute Seatbelt Limit:** Every passenger trip is strictly restricted to the registered manufacturer seatbelt capacity of the vehicle:
   - **Standard Saloon / Comfort Hatchback:** Maximum of four (4) passengers (one in front, three in the rear);
   - **XL / 7-Seater Utility:** Maximum of six (6) passengers;
   - **Boda (Motorcycle):** Maximum of one (1) pillion passenger.
2. **Prohibition of Overcrowding:** Passengers are strictly prohibited from forcing, cajoling, or coercing drivers to carry excess occupants or to permit unbelted children to sit on adult laps. Carrying excess passengers constitutes a serious criminal traffic offense under **Section 100 of the Traffic Act (Cap 403)** exposing the driver to criminal arrest, court arraignment, and driver's license suspension.
3. **Right to Cancel Overcrowded Bookings:** A driver partner has the mandatory duty to refuse carriage to any group exceeding the vehicle's licensed seatbelt capacity. In such event, the driver shall cancel the trip under the classification "Excess Passengers", and a standard cancellation fee shall be assessed against the passenger.

### 5.3 Luggage Limits and Absolute Prohibition of Commercial Cargo
1. **Personal Passenger Luggage Only:** Vehicles operating on the Cabi Africa network are licensed exclusively for private passenger carriage and personal travel luggage. Passengers may only carry personal bags, suitcases, or work equipment that fit comfortably within the vehicle's enclosed trunk/boot without obstructing the driver's rear-view mirror.
2. **Commercial Freight and Heavy Cargo Ban:** Passengers are strictly prohibited from utilizing passenger vehicles for transporting heavy commercial goods, bulk agricultural produce (such as sacks of potatoes, onions, or grain), building materials (cement, timber, iron sheets), greasy motor vehicle spare parts, or commercial wholesale inventory.
3. **Driver Right of Refusal:** A driver partner retains the complete right to decline any trip involving commercial cargo, overloaded boots that cause the vehicle chassis to sag, or items that risk tearing or dirtying the interior upholstery.

### 5.4 Safe Pickup Points and Prohibition on Traffic Violation Coercion
1. **Lawful Pickup Locations:** Passengers must designate safe, accessible, and legally authorized pickup spots. Passengers shall not instruct drivers to stop in designated red curbs, bus rapid transit lanes, highway acceleration lanes, roundabouts, or private property gated without entry clearance.
2. **The Driver as Master of the Vehicle:** The driver partner is the sole master and captain of the vehicle and exercises exclusive discretion regarding vehicle speed, lane discipline, and safe stopping spots.
3. **No Coercion to Break Traffic Rules:** Passengers are strictly prohibited from urging, pressuring, demanding, or bribing drivers to:
   - Exceed statutory speed limits;
   - Overlap, drive on road shoulders, or traverse pedestrian sidewalks;
   - Disregard traffic lights, stop signs, or one-way street markings;
   - Stop or wait in yellow curb zones or restricted County Council (*Kanjo*) enforcement areas.
4. **Indemnity for Passenger-Forced Violations:** Any traffic fine, Nairobi County towing fee, wheel-clamping penalty, or vehicle impoundment surcharge incurred by a driver partner resulting directly from complying with a passenger's persistent demand to stop in an unlawful zone shall be billed directly to the passenger's account, and the passenger covenants to fully indemnify the driver and Cabi Africa.

### 5.5 Waiting Periods and No-Show Cancellations
A complimentary waiting grace period of **five (5) minutes** commences upon the driver's arrival at the designated pickup point. Following the grace period, automated per-minute waiting tariffs apply. If the passenger fails to appear, fails to respond to phone calls/messages, or is not ready within ten (10) minutes of driver arrival, the driver may cancel the ride as a "Passenger No-Show", triggering a standard no-show cancellation fee.

### 5.6 Anti-Sabotage and Competitor Agent Interference Ban
CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED enforces strict electronic surveillance against platform sabotage. Any individual, competitor agent, or syndication attempting to manipulate the platform by issuing fraudulent phantom bookings, ordering and repeatedly cancelling rides to tie up driver supply, or orchestrating coordinated harassment against Cabi drivers commits a criminal offense under **Section 14 of the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018** (System Interference) and unfair trade practices under the **Competition Act (Cap 504)**. Such accounts shall be permanently blacklisted, IP and device hardware addresses blocked, and formal criminal complaints lodged with the DCI Cybercrime Unit and the Competition Authority of Kenya (CAK).

## 7. PASSENGER DON'TS: STRICTLY PROHIBITED CONDUCT & OFFENSES

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited. Engaging in any of these acts constitutes a material breach of contract, resulting in immediate ride termination, permanent account deactivation, financial penalties, and criminal prosecution:

### 7.1 Consumption of Food, Greasy Snacks and Open Beverages Inside the Vehicle
1. **General Food Prohibition:** Passengers are strictly prohibited from consuming meals, fast food, greasy snacks (fried chicken, chips, sausages, samosas), sticky confectioneries, ice cream, or open cups of soup, tea, or coffee inside the vehicle without the express prior permission of the driver partner.
2. **Permissible Exception:** Passengers may consume sealed, screw-capped bottled water, provided it is handled responsibly without spillage.
3. **Pest and Hygiene Safeguards:** Dropping food crumbs, discarding wrappers, or leaving food remains that attract pests or create foul interior odors is strictly prohibited.

### 7.2 Zero-Tolerance Prohibition on Alcohol, Miraa/Muguka, and Smoking/Vaping
1. **Alcoholic Beverages Ban:** Consuming alcohol, carrying open alcoholic cans/bottles, or entering the vehicle in an uncontrollable state of intoxication is strictly prohibited under the **Alcoholic Drinks Control Act, 2010**. Drivers are legally empowered to refuse or terminate carriage immediately upon detecting open alcohol.
2. **Miraa and Muguka Ban:** Chewing *miraa*, *muguka*, or spitting stimulants inside the vehicle or out of the windows is strictly banned.
3. **Smoking and E-Cigarettes Ban:** Smoking tobacco cigarettes, cannabis, cigars, electronic cigarettes, or vaping devices inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited under the **Tobacco Control Act (No. 4 of 2007)**.

### 7.3 Verbal Abuse, Sexual Harassment and Intimidation
Zero tolerance is enforced for abusive language, tribal slurs, vulgar comments, unwanted romantic advances, unsolicited comments on physical appearance, indecent exposure, or intentional physical contact. Sexual harassment will result in immediate police referral and permanent blacklisting.

### 7.4 Physical Assault and Vehicle Control Interference
Assaulting, striking, spitting upon, or physically menacing a driver partner, or tampering with the steering wheel, ignition key, gear selector, handbrake, pedals, or dashboard controls while the vehicle is stationary or in motion is a severe criminal offense under the Penal Code and will result in immediate arrest.

### 7.5 Transportation of Illicit Goods, Contraband and Weapons
Passengers shall not introduce into any vehicle firearms, ammunition, dangerous weapons, explosives, flammable chemicals, corrosive acids, illegal narcotics, poached wildlife products, uninspected bushmeat, or stolen property. Drivers are legally instructed to drive directly into the nearest police station upon suspecting contraband carriage.

### 7.6 Domestic Pets and Livestock Restrictions
1. **Livestock and Poultry Ban:** Carrying live farm animals, poultry (chickens, ducks, turkeys), goats, or sheep inside the vehicle passenger cabin or boot is strictly prohibited.
2. **Domestic Pets:** Domestic cats and dogs are only permitted with the driver's explicit prior agreement, provided the pet is transported inside a clean, secure, leak-proof pet carrier.
3. **Certified Service Animals:** Guide dogs assisting passengers with visual or sensory disabilities are permitted pursuant to the Persons with Disabilities Act, provided the passenger informs the driver in advance to facilitate clean seating.

### 7.7 Malicious Social Media Defamation, Extortion and Doxxing
1. **Mandatory Internal Dispute Exhaustion:** The passenger covenants to submit any genuine customer dissatisfaction, fare discrepancy, or driver misconduct report through the official Cabi Africa Customer Care desk (**0758 777 222** / in-app support) for formal investigation and resolution prior to publishing external commentary.
2. **Prohibition on Social Media Extortion:** Threatening a driver or the Company with defamatory social media campaigns (e.g., *"give me a free ride/refund or I will destroy your brand on Twitter/TikTok"*) is strictly illegal.
3. **Cyber Harassment and Criminal Defamation:** Publishing false, malicious, unverified, or fabricated allegations, or publicly exposing a driver's national ID, home address, or family details (doxxing) constitutes a serious offense under **Section 23 of the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018** (Publishing false information) punishable by up to ten (10) years imprisonment or a KES 5,000,000 fine, in addition to civil liability under the **Defamation Act (Cap 36)**.

### 7.8 Unauthorized Recording and Privacy Leaks
Secretly recording video or audio of a driver partner or publishing unedited in-cabin interactions on social media platforms without consent violates **Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019** (Unlawful disclosure of personal data) and shall result in immediate account deletion and civil damages.

## 9. FARES, DRIVER DIRECT COLLECTION, CLEANING FEES AND PENALTIES

### 9.1 Transparent Tariff Calculation
Upfront fares displayed in the application are computed using an automated tariff algorithm comprising:
a. **Base Fare:** Initial dispatch flat charge;  
b. **Distance Rate:** Standard charge per kilometer traveled;  
c. **Time Rate:** Standard charge per minute of journey duration;  
d. **Statutory Levies and Road Tolls:** Applicable Nairobi Expressway tolls or statutory levies; and  
e. **Applicable Taxes:** Statutory Value Added Tax (VAT) where mandated by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA).

### 9.2 Driver Direct Fare Collection Mandate
1. **Direct Consideration to Driver:** In accordance with Cabi Africa’s operating model, the driver partner operates as an independent contractor directly authorized and responsible for collecting the gross trip fare from the passenger upon reaching the agreed destination.
2. **Approved Direct Payment Methods:** Passengers shall tender payment directly to the driver via:
   - **Cash (Kenyan Shillings):** In exact currency notes;
   - **Driver Mobile Money:** Direct transfer to the driver partner’s registered M-Pesa Buy Goods Till Number, Paybill, or personal mobile number as verified on the app; or
   - **In-App Digital Wallet / Credit Card:** Where pre-authorized by the passenger's payment profile.
3. **Automated Electronic Receipt:** Upon trip completion and fare confirmation, the platform automatically issues a detailed electronic receipt to the passenger’s registered email address and in-app trip history.

### 9.3 Strict Penalties for Fare Evasion and Fraudulent M-Pesa Reversals
1. **Statutory Offenses:** Boarding a vehicle, receiving transportation services, and subsequently refusing to pay the lawful fare, escaping from the vehicle without settling the bill, or initiating a fraudulent, bad-faith M-Pesa reversal constitutes:
   - **Obtaining Credit by False Pretenses** under **Section 316A of the Penal Code (Cap 63)**;
   - **Electronic Payment Fraud** under **Section 35 of the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018**; and
   - A direct civil debt owed to the driver partner and Cabi Africa.
2. **Enforcement and Blacklisting:** Where a passenger evades a fare or initiates an unauthorized mobile money reversal:
   - The outstanding debt shall be immediately logged against the passenger's national identity profile and phone number;
   - Any future booking attempted from that account, device, or phone number shall be blocked until the arrears plus a 20% administrative recovery fee are settled;
   - Cabi Africa reserves the full right to report the passenger's details to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) and pursue civil debt recovery in the Small Claims Court.

### 9.4 Cancellation Fees
A standard cancellation fee is payable by the passenger where:
a. The passenger cancels a ride more than **three (3) minutes** after driver acceptance; or  
b. The passenger fails to show up within the designated ten (10) minute waiting window.  
*Cancellation fees are automatically waived if the driver’s arrival is delayed by more than ten (10) minutes beyond the initial estimated arrival time.*

### 9.5 Cleaning, Spillage, and Interior Soiling Fees
1. **Passenger Liability for Cleanliness:** Every passenger is legally and financially responsible for maintaining the cleanliness and sanitation of the vehicle's interior upholstery, carpets, and door panels.
2. **Standardized Cleaning Fee Schedule:** Where a passenger spills liquids, drops food grease, leaves pungent odors, vomits, or Soils the vehicle interior requiring commercial detailing and causing the driver downtime:
   - **Minor Food / Liquid Spills:** Liquid soda, water stains, food crumbs requiring vacuuming: **KES 1,000 to KES 1,500**;
   - **Major Food Greases / Sticky Sauces / Animal Mess:** Sticky syrups, tomato sauces, grease, pet urine requiring chemical shampooing: **KES 2,000 to KES 3,000**;
   - **Vomiting / Bodily Fluids / Pungent Odor Decontamination:** Vomit, blood, or severe alcohol messes requiring deep foam extraction, odor ozone treatment, and minimum 24-hour vehicle grounding: **KES 3,500 to KES 5,000**.
3. **Assessment and Collection:** The driver partner must submit photographic evidence and a commercial car wash / detailing receipt to Cabi Africa within twelve (12) hours. Once verified, the fee is billed directly to the passenger's payment profile or debited from their account before any subsequent ride can be dispatched.
