## 1. PREAMBLE & CORE VALUES
**CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED** (Company Reg. No. **PVT-OD169L8L**), trading as **Cabi Africa**, is dedicated to providing safe, reliable, dignified, and lawful digital ride-hailing services across Kenya. 

The Cabi Africa community is anchored on five foundational pillars:
1. **Uncompromised Safety & Security:** Prioritizing personal safety and vehicle integrity above commercial speed.
2. **Mutual Dignity & Respect:** Zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, hostility, or condescension.
3. **Statutory Integrity & Lawfulness:** Strict compliance with Kenyan national statutes, county by-laws, and road safety regulations.
4. **Data Privacy & Confidentiality:** Respecting personal boundaries and statutory rights under the Data Protection Act, 2019.
5. **Operational Accountability:** Transparent, evidence-based dispute resolution and progressive disciplinary fairness.

Every user accessing the Cabi Africa digital platform—whether as a Driver Partner, Vehicle Owner, Commuter/Passenger, or Operational Staff—is contractually and legally bound to uphold this Safety Charter.

## 2. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR DRIVER PARTNERS

### 2.1. Road Safety, Defensive Driving & Physical Fitness
* **Zero Intoxication (Absolute BAC 0.00%):** A driver partner shall never operate a vehicle or log into the driver application while under the influence of alcohol, recreational drugs, prescription medications causing drowsiness, or *miraa/muguka*. Zero tolerance is strictly enforced.
* **Speed Limits & Traffic Law Compliance:** Adhere strictly to the Traffic Act (Cap 403) speed limits: maximum 50 km/h in urban/residential zones, 100 km/h on dual-carriageway highways, or as posted by road signage. Reckless overtaking, running red traffic lights, overlapping on shoulders, driving on pedestrian walkways, or driving against oncoming traffic is strictly prohibited.
* **Distraction-Free Cabin:** Mobile devices must be securely mounted on an approved dashboard or windshield cradle. Texting, browsing social media, viewing videos, or holding phone conversations without hands-free equipment while moving is prohibited.
* **Seatbelt & Crash Helmet Compliance:** The driver partner must ensure that all vehicle occupants buckle up before starting vehicle movement. In motorcycle hailing operations, drivers must provide an approved, sanitized crash helmet and reflector vest for the passenger.
* **Statutory Fatigue Management (12-Hour Daily Cap):** To prevent driver exhaustion and road accidents, no driver shall be logged on or actively dispatching for more than twelve (12) cumulative hours within a 24-hour cycle. The application enforces a mandatory six (6) hour offline rest period following 12 cumulative hours of active service.

### 2.2. Professionalism, Non-Discrimination & Passenger Service
* **Equality & Universal Inclusivity:** Serve all passengers without discrimination based on ethnicity, tribe, gender, religion, race, national origin, political affiliation, physical disability, sexual orientation, or age.
* **Assistance to Vulnerable Commuters:** Proactively assist elderly commuters, expectant mothers, and persons with disabilities. Certified guide dogs and mobility aids (wheelchairs, crutches, walking frames) must be accommodated without levying surcharge fees.
* **Respect for Personal Space & Boundaries:** Professional decorum must be maintained throughout the ride. Drivers must refrain from asking probing personal questions regarding marital status, residence, sexual orientation, or personal finances. Uninvited physical contact, flirtatious remarks, or inappropriate staring are grounds for immediate police reporting and permanent platform expulsion.
* **Strict Post-Trip Disconnection:** Never call, text, WhatsApp, follow on social media, stalk, or visit a passenger after the trip has concluded. Passenger contact data revealed via in-app masked routing is strictly privileged under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

### 2.3. In-Cabin Camera & Dashcam Privacy Ethics
* **Approved Visual Decal Requirement:** Driver partners operating safety dashcams inside the vehicle must display the mandatory in-cabin passenger notice decal (*"Notice: For your safety and security, this vehicle is equipped with an in-cabin audio-visual recording device"*).
* **Strict Anti-Doxxing & Social Media Leak Ban:** In-cabin dashcam recordings are recorded exclusively for crime prevention, accident reconstruction, and official police investigations. Any extraction, export, or posting of footage to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, WhatsApp, or any public forum constitutes an egregious violation of Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, triggering immediate lifetime deactivation, civil indemnity, and criminal prosecution.

## 3. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR PASSENGERS & COMMUTERS

### 3.1. Verified Account Identity & Profile Integrity
* **Authentic Profile Avatar:** Every commuter must register with their genuine legal name and maintain an authentic, recognizable facial photo avatar on their in-app profile. The use of cartoon avatars, vehicle pictures, landscapes, celebrity portraits, or deceptive pseudonyms is prohibited.
* **Driver’s Right of Visual Authentication:** For driver physical safety and to deter armed robberies, ambushes, or ride-hailing identity theft, driver partners possess the legal right to visually verify the passenger's face against their in-app profile avatar before unlocking cabin doors.
* **Periodic Biometric & OTP Verification:** Commuters must comply with periodic identity confirmations (SMS/WhatsApp OTP, facial selfie liveness verification, or device biometrics), particularly during nocturnal hours (10:00 PM – 5:00 AM) or bookings originating within designated high-risk geographic coordinates.

### 3.2. Respect for Drivers & Traffic Legality
* **Civil Demeanor & Courtesy:** Treat driver partners with professional dignity. Shouting, insults, vulgar language, abusive hand gestures, threats of violence, or physical damage to vehicle fixtures (such as door slamming, kicking seats, tampering with dashboards) will lead to immediate account suspension and financial liability for repairs.
* **No Unlawful Demands or Traffic Violations:** Passengers shall not coerce, command, or bribe drivers to violate Kenyan road traffic laws. Demands to speed, overlap in traffic jams, enter pedestrian zones, drive on dedicated Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) lanes, or make illegal U-turns are strictly forbidden.
* **No Overloading:** Under Section 100 of the Traffic Act (Cap 403), saloon vehicles are strictly certified for a maximum of four (4) passengers. Passengers shall not demand to carry excess passengers or place unrestrained children on adult laps. Drivers are mandated to refuse overloaded trips without cancellation penalties.
* **Prohibited Stopping & County Clamping Indemnity:** Passengers shall not compel drivers to stop, wait, or drop them off in designated red zones, yellow curbs, bus stages, or prohibited county enforcement areas. Any county parking clamping, towing, or impound fines directly occasioned by a passenger’s insistence shall be fully surcharged to the passenger’s account.

### 3.3. Cabin Hygiene, Strict Cleanliness & Intoxicant Prohibition
* **Strict Eating & Open Beverage Ban:** To preserve sanitary standards and safeguard vehicle upholstery, eating meals, hot food, fast food, snacks, or consuming open beverages inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited. Only factory-sealed bottled drinking water is permitted.
* **Absolute Ban on Intoxicants & Smoking:** Smoking tobacco, using electronic cigarettes (vaping), chewing *miraa/khat/muguka*, and consuming open alcoholic drinks inside the cabin are strictly prohibited under any circumstances. Drivers possess the non-penalized right to immediately terminate the trip at the nearest safe, lit public location if a passenger refuses compliance.
* **Commercial Detailing & Soiling Surcharge:** Accidental or negligent soiling of the vehicle interior (vomiting, drink spills, food grease, muddy dirt, pet dander) attracts an immediate, non-negotiable professional cleaning fee ranging between **KES 1,500 and KES 3,500**, assessed by the Trust & Safety team and reimbursed directly to the affected driver.

### 3.4. Commercial Cargo vs. Personal Commuter Luggage
* **Permissible Personal Luggage:** Passengers may transport standard travel bags, briefcases, suitcases, grocery bags, and personal electronics that comfortably fit within the vehicle’s luggage boot or under footwells without obstructing vehicle windows or driving visibility.
* **Prohibited Cargo:** Transporting commercial merchandise, construction hardware (timber, cement bags, iron sheets), foul-smelling agricultural produce, raw meat/fish, petrol/gas cylinders, flammable chemicals, livestock, or unrestrained pets is strictly prohibited. Drivers have the absolute right to cancel rides involving hazardous or prohibited cargo.

## 4. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE OFFENSES (UNIVERSAL APPLICATION)

The following violations carry zero tolerance across all drivers, vehicle owners, and passengers, resulting in immediate suspension, permanent platform expulsion, and formal referral to the National Police Service (DCI / Traffic Department):

1. **Physical Violence, Assault & Weapons:**
   * Any act of physical violence, battery, threatening behavior, or brandishing of firearms, blades, blunt weapons, or explosive devices.
2. **Sexual Misconduct & Gender-Based Violence (GBV):**
   * Non-consensual sexual advances, unsolicited physical contact, explicit sexual remarks, indecent exposure, or soliciting commercial sexual services.
3. **Off-Platform Solicitation & Fare Poaching:**
   * Proposing, soliciting, or agreeing to cancel active platform trip dispatches to conduct unrecorded cash-in-hand transactions ("off-app trips"). Off-app transactions void third-party passenger liability insurance and GPS panic tracking, resulting in permanent blacklisting.
4. **Fare Evasion & Fraudulent Mobile Money Reversals:**
   * Refusal to pay authorized trip fares, or maliciously initiating fraudulent M-Pesa transaction reversals following completed rides (a criminal offense under Section 316A of the Penal Code, Cap 63).
5. **Cyber Extortion, Review Blackmail & Defamation:**
   * Threatening negative 1-star ratings, lodging fabricated complaints, or threatening social media character assassination to coerce free rides, extortionate cash payouts, or personal favors (sanctioned under Sections 23 and 27 of the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018).
6. **Data Privacy Breaches & Doxxing:**
   * Publishing private passenger or driver phone numbers, residences, names, photographs, or dashcam footage on public digital channels without statutory court orders or ODPC authorization.

## 5. ENFORCEMENT PROTOCOL & PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINE

Violations of this Community Code of Conduct are adjudicated through Cabi’s Trust & Safety Operations Center under an objective evidentiary standard:

| Severity Level | Categorization of Offense | Disciplinary Sanction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Minor Operational** | Minor cabin cleanliness disputes, slight discourtesy, minor route disagreement | Written advisory, customer service coaching, performance warning |
| **Tier 2: Repeated / Moderate** | Repeated cancelations, minor vehicle soiling refusal, discourteous language | 24 to 72-hour temporary platform suspension, mandatory safety retraining |
| **Tier 3: Serious Violation** | Overloading insistence, verbal threats, abusive slurs, off-app trip solicitation | 7-day to 14-day suspension, final warning, administrative reset surcharge |
| **Tier 4: Critical Safety Breach** | Physical assault, GBV, weapon possession, DUI, dashcam footage leak, fare fraud | Immediate permanent deactivation, lifetime blacklisting, police referral |

### Independent Appeals Process:
Any user subjected to a Tier 2, Tier 3, or Tier 4 disciplinary action maintains the statutory right to lodge an appeal within **fourteen (14) calendar days** before the **Cabi Independent Appeals Committee (IAC)**, which shall review GPS telematics, audio-visual dashcam logs, and witness statements to issue a binding determination.
