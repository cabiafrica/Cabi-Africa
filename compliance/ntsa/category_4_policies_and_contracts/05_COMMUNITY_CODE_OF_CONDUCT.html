COMMUNITY CODE OF CONDUCT & SAFETY CHARTER

Mandatory Operating Ethics, Safety Standards & Mutual Rights for Drivers, Passengers, Vehicle Owners and Operations Staff
Statutory Authority: NTSA (TNC) Regulations, 2022 | Traffic Act (Cap 403) | Data Protection Act, 2019 | Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018 | Penal Code (Cap 63)

1. PREAMBLE & CORE VALUES

CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED (trading as Cabi Africa) is committed to building the safest, most lawful, and professionally dignified digital ride-hailing ecosystem across the Republic of Kenya. This Community Code of Conduct and Safety Charter establishes non-negotiable behavioral boundaries for every individual participating on the platform.

Our community values are founded on five inviolable tenets:

  1. Uncompromised Safety & Security: The sanctity of human life, physical welfare, and vehicle safety supersedes commercial convenience or speed.
  2. Mutual Dignity & Professional Respect: Zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, hostility, condescension, or abusive behavior across all platform interactions.
  3. Absolute Statutory Lawfulness: Full compliance with Kenyan traffic statutes, national penal laws, municipal county by-laws, and NTSA regulatory directives.
  4. Data Privacy & Anti-Doxxing Integrity: Strict protection of personal contact data, telemetry, and camera footage under the Data Protection Act, 2019.
  5. Operational Fairness & Due Process: Objective evidentiary assessment of customer disputes, transparent progressive discipline, and impartial appeal rights.

2. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR DRIVER PARTNERS

2.1. Road Safety, Defensive Driving & Physical Fitness

  • Zero Intoxication (Absolute BAC 0.00%): A driver partner shall never operate a vehicle or log into the driver application while under the influence of alcohol, recreational narcotics, prescription sedatives, or miraa/muguka. Zero blood alcohol concentration is strictly enforced at all times.
  • Traffic Discipline & Speed Limits: Adhere strictly to the Traffic Act (Cap 403) speed limits: 50 km/h in urban centers, 100 km/h on designated highways, or as posted by road signs. Reckless overtaking, jumping red lights, sidewalk driving, or overlapping on road shoulders is strictly prohibited.
  • Distraction-Free Cockpit: Mobile devices must be securely mounted on an approved dashboard cradle. Texting, browsing feeds, or handheld phone calls while operating the vehicle is strictly forbidden.
  • Restraint & Helmet Enforcement: Drivers must ensure all occupants buckle up before moving. On motorcycle hailing trips, drivers must provide a certified crash helmet and reflector vest for the commuter.
  • Statutory 12-Hour Fatigue Cap: To prevent driver fatigue and catastrophic collisions, no driver partner shall drive for more than twelve (12) cumulative hours within a 24-hour cycle. The platform enforces a mandatory six (6) hour offline rest period following 12 cumulative hours of service.

2.2. Professional Courtesy, Inclusivity & Boundary Ethics

  • Universal Inclusivity: Drivers must serve all commuters equitably without discrimination based on ethnicity, tribe, gender, religion, race, disability, or age.
  • Assistance to Vulnerable Commuters: Proactively assist elderly commuters, expectant mothers, and persons with disabilities. Wheelchairs, crutches, and certified service guide dogs must be transported at zero extra charge.
  • Respect for Personal Space: Drivers must maintain professional decorum. Inappropriate staring, flirtatious commentary, probing personal inquiries (regarding marital status, dating life, or finances), or unsolicited physical touching are strictly forbidden and warrant immediate platform deactivation and criminal referral.
  • Strict Post-Trip Disconnection: Never call, text, WhatsApp, stalk, or visit a commuter after trip conclusion. Passenger contact details are strictly confidential under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

2.3. In-Cabin Camera & Privacy Ethics

  • Mandatory In-Cabin Warning Decal: Driver partners deploying safety dashcams inside the vehicle must display the mandatory visible passenger notification decal.
  • Strict Anti-Doxxing & Social Media Ban: In-cabin dashcam recordings are preserved solely for official accident reconstruction and police investigations. Any export or sharing of footage on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, or WhatsApp constitutes a grave breach of Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019, triggering instant lifetime blacklisting, civil liability, and criminal prosecution.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR PASSENGERS & COMMUTERS

3.1. Verified Account Identity & Profile Integrity

  • Authentic Profile Avatar: Every passenger must register with their genuine legal name and maintain an authentic, recognizable passport-style portrait avatar on their in-app profile. The use of cartoon graphics, landscapes, celebrities, or pseudonyms is strictly prohibited.
  • Driver's Visual Verification Right: For driver personal safety against violent robberies and carjackings, drivers maintain the legal right to visually match the commuter’s face against their in-app profile avatar before unlocking cabin doors.
  • Security Checks: Commuters must comply with periodic security verification checks (SMS OTP, facial liveness confirmation) during nocturnal hours (10:00 PM – 5:00 AM) or bookings originating from designated high-risk coordinates.

3.2. Respect for Drivers & Traffic Legality

  • Civil Demeanor: Treat driver partners with professional dignity. Verbal abuse, shouting, insults, physical threats, slamming doors, or damaging vehicle upholstery will result in immediate passenger account suspension and repair liabilities.
  • No Unlawful Demands: Passengers shall not coerce, pressure, or bribe drivers into violating road traffic regulations (e.g., speeding, overlapping in traffic, making illegal U-turns, or driving on dedicated bus lanes).
  • No Overloading (Section 100 Traffic Act): Standard saloon cars are legally licensed for a maximum of four (4) passengers. Passengers shall not demand to squeeze excess commuters or carry unrestrained children on adult laps. Drivers are mandated to refuse overloaded trips without cancellation penalties.
  • Prohibited Stopping & County Clamping Indemnity: Passengers shall not compel drivers to stop, wait, or drop off in prohibited red zones, yellow curbs, or county enforcement areas. Any county parking clamping, towing, or impound fines occasioned by a passenger’s insistence shall be fully charged to the passenger’s account.

3.3. Cabin Cleanliness, Food & Intoxicant Prohibitions

  • Strict Eating & Open Beverage Ban: To maintain vehicle cleanliness and hygiene, eating meals, hot food, fast food, snacks, or consuming open drinks inside the vehicle is strictly prohibited. Only factory-sealed bottled water is permitted.
  • Absolute Ban on Smoking, Vaping, Alcohol & Miraa: Smoking tobacco, using electronic cigarettes (vaping), chewing miraa/muguka, and consuming open alcoholic beverages inside the vehicle are strictly prohibited. Drivers have the non-penalized right to terminate the trip immediately at the nearest safe public location if a passenger refuses compliance.
  • Commercial Detailing & Soiling Surcharge: Accidental or negligent interior soiling (vomit, drink spills, grease, mud, pet dander) attracts an immediate, non-negotiable cleaning fee between KES 1,500 and KES 3,500, reimbursed directly to the affected driver partner.

3.4. Luggage Standards vs. Prohibited Cargo

  • Permissible Personal Luggage: Standard suitcases, travel bags, briefcases, and shopping items that fit safely into the luggage boot without obstructing driver visibility are permitted.
  • Prohibited Commercial Cargo: Transporting construction hardware (timber, cement, iron sheets), foul-smelling agricultural commodities, raw meat/fish, petrol/gas cylinders, flammable chemicals, livestock, or unrestrained animals is strictly prohibited.

4. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE OFFENSES (UNIVERSAL APPLICATION)

The following violations carry zero tolerance across all drivers, vehicle owners, and passengers, resulting in immediate suspension, permanent platform expulsion, and formal referral to the National Police Service (DCI / Traffic Department):

  1. Physical Assault, Violence & Weapons: Battery, physical aggression, fighting, brandishing knives, firearms, or blunt weapons.
  2. Sexual Misconduct & Gender-Based Violence (GBV): Unsolicited touching, lewd sexual remarks, indecent exposure, stalking, or sexual solicitations.
  3. Off-Platform Fare Poaching: Proposing or accepting off-app cash deals ("cancel the app and pay cash"). Off-app rides void passenger liability insurance and GPS emergency tracking, resulting in permanent blacklisting.
  4. Fare Evasion & M-Pesa Reversal Fraud: Non-payment of trip fares or maliciously initiating mobile money reversals following ride completion (a criminal offense under Section 316A of the Penal Code).
  5. Cyber Extortion & Review Blackmail: Threatening 1-star ratings, false safety reports, or social media defamation to coerce free rides or extortionate payouts (Sections 23 and 27, Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018).
  6. Data Breaches & Doxxing: Disclosing private phone numbers, trip routes, residential addresses, or passenger/driver dashcam footage on social networks.

5. PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES & DUE PROCESS

The Cabi Trust & Safety Operations Center enforces compliance under a structured, evidence-based disciplinary framework:

Severity Tier Classification of Infraction Disciplinary & Remedial Action
Tier 1: Minor Operational Minor cleanliness disputes, slight discourtesy, minor route disagreements Written formal advisory, customer service coaching, performance warning
Tier 2: Moderate Breach Repeated cancellations, refusal to pay soiling fee, discourteous language 24 to 72-hour temporary platform suspension, mandatory safety refresher
Tier 3: Serious Violation Overloading insistence, verbal threats, abusive slurs, off-app trip poaching 7 to 14-day suspension, final warning, administrative reset surcharge
Tier 4: Critical Safety Assault, GBV, weapons, DUI, dashcam footage leaks, mobile money fare fraud Immediate permanent deactivation, lifetime blacklisting, DCI police referral

Independent Appeals Process:

Any driver partner or commuter subjected to a Tier 2, Tier 3, or Tier 4 deactivation maintains the statutory right to appeal within fourteen (14) calendar days before the Cabi Independent Appeals Committee (IAC). The IAC convenes weekly to examine GPS telematics, dashcam logs, and witness statements, rendering a binding determination within seven (7) business days.
