COMMUNITY CODE OF CONDUCT & SAFETY CHARTER

(MANDATORY OPERATING ETHICS FOR DRIVERS, PASSENGERS & OPERATORS)

REGULATORY FRAMEWORK: NTSA TNC REGULATIONS, 2022 | DATA PROTECTION ACT, 2019 | TRAFFIC ACT (CAP 403) | PENAL CODE OF KENYA

1. OUR CORE VALUES

The Cabi Africa community is built on four core pillars: Safety, Mutual Respect, Integrity, and Professional Courtesy. Every individual using our platform—whether driving, riding, or managing vehicles—is expected to uphold these foundational standards.

2. STANDARDS FOR DRIVER PARTNERS

2.1. Road Safety & Defensive Driving

  • Zero Intoxication: Never operate a vehicle under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or prescription medications that impair reflexes. Zero blood alcohol concentration (BAC) is enforced.
  • Speed & Traffic Discipline: Adhere strictly to Kenyan traffic speed limits (50 km/h in urban areas, 100 km/h on highways, or as signposted). Reckless overtaking, overlapping on sidewalks/shoulders, and jumping red lights are strictly prohibited.
  • Distraction-Free Cab: Mobile phones must be securely mounted on an approved dashboard or windshield cradle. Texting, browsing social media, or watching video media while driving is prohibited.
  • Mandatory Seatbelt & Helmet Use: Ensure all passengers buckle up before commencing movement (or wear certified crash helmets on commercial motorcycle trips).
  • Maximum 12-Hour Driving Limit: Drivers must observe statutory rest rules, taking at least six (6) hours offline after twelve (12) cumulative hours of driving to prevent driver fatigue.

2.2. Professionalism, Non-Discrimination & Courtesy

  • Equality & Inclusivity: Serve all passengers without discrimination based on race, tribe, gender, religion, nationality, disability, sexual orientation, or age.
  • Service Animals & Persons with Disabilities: Welcome passengers with disabilities, guide dogs, wheelchairs, or crutches with dignity and proactive assistance without charging additional fees.
  • Privacy & Boundary Respect: Never initiate flirtatious, suggestive, or overly personal conversation. Unsolicited physical contact is grounds for immediate police reporting and permanent platform expulsion.
  • Post-Trip Disconnection: Never call, message, stalk, or visit a passenger after the trip has concluded. Passenger contact data is strictly confidential under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

2.3. In-Cabin Camera & Privacy Ethics

  • Drivers deploying approved in-cabin safety dashcams must display the required passenger warning decal inside the cabin.
  • In-cabin recordings must never be downloaded, exported, or posted to social media (TikTok, YouTube, X, WhatsApp, Facebook). Breaching passenger privacy carries immediate termination, criminal prosecution under Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, and legal indemnity.

3. STANDARDS FOR PASSENGERS

3.1. Mandatory Profile Identity & Verification

  • Verifiable Profile Avatar: Every passenger must register with their genuine full legal name and upload a clear, recognizable passport-standard facial photo to serve as their in-app profile avatar.
  • Periodic Biometric & Security Verifications: To protect driver safety against ambush, robberies, and fake accounts, passengers must comply with periodic identity checks (via SMS/WhatsApp OTP, facial selfie liveness confirmation, or device biometrics) before booking, especially during night hours or high-risk zones.
  • Right of Driver Visual Confirmation: Drivers have the right to visually match the boarding passenger with their in-app profile avatar before unlocking doors or commencing the trip.

3.2. Safety & Respect for Drivers

  • Civil Conduct: Treat the driver with dignity and respect. Verbal abuse, shouting, aggressive gestures, or destruction of vehicle property will lead to passenger account suspension.
  • No Unlawful Demands: Never coerce the driver into exceeding passenger carrying capacity (e.g., carrying 5 passengers in a 4-seater car), speeding, making illegal U-turns, or driving on prohibited lanes.
  • Safety Restraints: Wear seatbelts at all times during the journey as required by the Traffic Act (or certified helmets on motorcycle trips).

3.3. Cleanliness & Vehicle Care

  • Do not smoke, vape, consume open alcoholic beverages, or consume messy food inside the vehicle without the express consent of the driver.
  • Take personal responsibility for waste and wrappers; leave the vehicle as clean as you found it.

4. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE POLICIES (APPLICABLE TO ALL)

  1. Physical Violence, Assault & Weapons:
  • Any physical aggression, assault, brandishing of weapons, knives, illegal narcotics, or explosives is strictly prohibited and results in immediate police arrest and lifetime ban.
  1. Sexual Harassment & Gender-Based Violence (GBV):
  • Verbal remarks of a sexual nature, inappropriate staring, uninvited touching, solicitation of sexual favors, or indecent exposure are zero-tolerance violations resulting in immediate account termination and handover to law enforcement.
  1. Fraud & Off-Platform Poaching:
  • Offering or soliciting off-app cash deals ("cancel the app and pay me directly") violates platform insurance coverage and results in permanent blacklisting of both driver and rider accounts.

5. ENFORCEMENT & PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES

  • Violations of this Code of Conduct are investigated through Cabi’s Trust & Safety Operations Center.
  • Minor operational infractions follow progressive discipline (warning, customer service coaching, 24-72h temporary cooldown, or administrative reset fee).
  • Severe safety breaches (violence, sexual harassment, criminal acts, camera recording leaks) result in immediate suspension, referral to the National Police Service, and permanent deactivation.
  • Any affected party has the right to appeal within fourteen (14) days before the Cabi Independent Appeals Committee.
