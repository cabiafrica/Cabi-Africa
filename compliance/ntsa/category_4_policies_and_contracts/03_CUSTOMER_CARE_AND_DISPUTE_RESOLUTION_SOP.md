## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory processes, operational protocols, investigation standards, and resolution timelines for handling inquiries, grievances, disputes, and emergency safety reports received from passengers, driver partners, vehicle owners, and regulatory authorities.

## 4. DETAILED OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES BY INCIDENT TYPE

### 4.1. Handling Tier 1 Critical Safety Incidents (SOS Button Activations)
1. **Trigger:** An SOS alert is triggered either via the in-app emergency button or an incoming high-priority emergency phone call.
2. **Instant Telemetry Audit:** The on-duty Security Operations Officer instantly isolates the trip on the live dispatch map, locking:
   * Current GPS coordinates and telemetry speed;
   * Full driver profile (photo, ID, PSV badge, phone number, vehicle plate);
   * Passenger profile and registered emergency trusted contact details.
3. **Outbound Verification Call:** The Safety Officer places an outbound emergency call to both passenger and driver within three (3) minutes to assess safety.
4. **Emergency Dispatch:** If distress is verified or if neither party answers after two attempts:
   * The nearest emergency security/medical response partner is dispatched to the live GPS location;
   * The incident is immediately reported to the National Police Service (NPS) 999/112 command center;
   * The driver's account is placed on an automatic administrative safety freeze pending formal investigation.
5. **Post-Incident Reporting:** A comprehensive written Incident Report is compiled and archived within twenty-four (24) hours for regulatory submission.

### 4.2. Handling Financial & Billing Disputes (18% Commission & Fare Audits)
1. **Log & Trace:** The billing agent inspects the trip’s digital telemetry log: initial estimated distance vs. actual GPS track driven.
2. **Route Detours & Mutual Agreement Protocol:**
   * **Unauthorized Detour:** If the driver took an unauthorized longer route without passenger consent or operational justification, the fare is recalculated using the optimal route distance, and the difference is credited to the passenger's wallet within two (2) hours.
   * **Mutually Agreed Alternative Route:** Where the passenger and driver mutually agreed to take an alternative or longer route (e.g., to bypass heavy traffic, road construction, flooding, for personal safety, or for passenger-requested intermediate stops), the actual recorded distance and fare remain valid. No refund or penalty shall be imposed on the driver.
3. **Commission Verification:** The platform audit ensures that Cabi's platform commission did not exceed 18% of the net fare.
4. **Notification:** Both driver and passenger receive a detailed digital breakdown explaining the adjustment.

### 4.3. Lost and Found Item Protocol
1. **Reporting:** Passenger logs a lost item report via the trip history screen within thirty (30) days of the ride.
2. **Resolution Options:**
   * **Option 1 (Primary - Passenger Delivery Service):** The passenger contacts the driver via masked in-app calling/chat and organizes a delivery service (via Cabi Delivery or authorized courier) to retrieve the item directly to the passenger's location at the passenger's expense.
   * **Option 2 (Secondary - Custody Handover):** If immediate return is not practical, the driver must surrender the item within twenty-four (24) hours to the Cabi Head Office Lost & Found Desk (Ridgeways Commercial Centre, Kiambu Road, Nairobi) or the nearest National Police Station.
3. **Custody & Retention Period at Cabi Office:**
   * Unclaimed valuables and personal effects are retained in secure custody at the Cabi Head Office for a maximum period of **ninety (90) days** in accordance with the Disposal of Uncollected Goods Act (Cap 38, Laws of Kenya).
   * Perishable goods (food, groceries) are documented and hygienically disposed of after twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) hours.
4. **Disposal / Escalation Upon Lapse of 90 Days:**
   * **Official & Identity Documents:** National IDs, Passports, Driving Licenses, and Bank Cards that remain unclaimed after 90 days are handed over to the National Registration Bureau, Immigration Department, or Central Police Station with a formal handover certificate.
   * **General Unclaimed Goods:** Goods remaining unclaimed after ninety (90) days are disposed of pursuant to the Disposal of Uncollected Goods Act (either through public auction with net proceeds remitted to the driver/company welfare fund, or donated to registered charitable homes).
5. **Chain of Custody & Inspection Authority (Who May Inspect):**
   * Opening and checking a surrendered lost item is strictly restricted to the **Designated Safety & Compliance Officer** in the mandatory presence of an **Independent Witness** (Security Supervisor or Customer Care Lead).
   * No personnel are permitted to inspect lost property alone.
   * A two-signatory **Lost Property Custody Sheet** must be completed, itemizing: item description, make, serial numbers (for phones, laptops, electronics), visible condition, and date/time of intake.
   * The item is immediately placed in a numbered, tamper-evident security bag and locked in the fireproof office safe.

## 5. ESCALATION TO NTSA AND STATUTORY REPORTING
5.1. **Mandatory Reporting to NTSA:** In accordance with the TNC Regulations, 2022, Cabi Africa shall submit a quarterly consolidated summary of all customer safety complaints, resolved disputes, and road safety incidents to the Director of Road Safety at NTSA.  
5.2. **External Dispute Resolution:** If a passenger or driver remains dissatisfied with Cabi’s internal resolution after exhausting all internal tiers, Cabi shall inform the complainant of their statutory right to lodge an escalation directly with the **NTSA Complaints Directorate** or the **Competition Authority of Kenya (CAK)** for consumer grievances.
