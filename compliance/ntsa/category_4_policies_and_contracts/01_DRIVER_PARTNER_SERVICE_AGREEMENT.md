### 3. NATURE OF THE RELATIONSHIP, SHIFTS & CABIN SAFETY CAMERAS
3.1. **Independent Contractor Arrangement:** The Parties expressly record their relationship as that of an independent service provider. The Driver is engaged as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall create any partnership, joint venture, employer-employee relationship, agency, or master-servant relationship under the Employment Act, 2007.  
3.2. **Freedom of Operation & Shift Scheduling:** The Driver retains general discretion regarding when to log onto the Cabi Platform. However, in compliance with road safety standards, fatigue prevention rules, and market equilibrium:
* The Company reserves the right to implement operational shift windows, zone allocation quotas, and active online session limits to manage the number of concurrent online drivers on the network.
* In adherence to international and national road safety standards, no Driver shall remain on continuous active driving status exceeding twelve (12) consecutive hours without a mandatory rest window of at least six (6) consecutive hours.  
3.3. **Exclusivity:** This Agreement is non-exclusive; the Driver is free to engage in other lawful commercial pursuits provided they do not compromise active trip safety or violate platform confidentiality.  
3.4. **Passenger Verification Layer for Driver Safety:** To protect drivers from carjackings, violent crimes, and fraudulent bookings, the Company mandates that all passengers register with a verified, recognizable passport-standard facial photo as their profile avatar and undergo periodic identity authentications (via OTP, selfie capture, or other biometric authentications). The Driver is entitled to visually inspect and confirm that the boarding passenger matches the displayed profile avatar before commencing a trip.  
3.5. **In-Cabin Dashcams and Safety Recording Systems:**
* **Authorization:** To enhance on-trip safety and deter criminal conduct, the Driver is permitted and encouraged to install dual-facing safety dashcams (recording road and cabin) inside the Vehicle.
* **Mandatory Passenger Notice Decal:** Any Vehicle equipped with an in-cabin camera system must prominently display a physical notice sticker inside the passenger cabin stating:  
  *“Notice: For your safety and security, this vehicle is equipped with an in-cabin camera system. Recordings are encrypted and accessed solely for lawful incident and dispute investigations.”*
* **Automatic In-App Indicator:** The Cabi Platform shall inform passengers upon match that the assigned vehicle is equipped with an in-cabin safety recording device.
* **Tamper-Evident Loop Storage:** Recordings must operate on an automated rolling loop overwriting every forty-eight (48) to seventy-two (72) hours, unless an active safety incident or police inquiry is triggered.
* **Strict Anti-Leak & Social Media Prohibition:** In strict compliance with Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 and constitutional privacy rights, the Driver covenants that they shall **never** download, screenshot, share, or upload any passenger audio or video recordings to social media platforms (including TikTok, YouTube, X/Twitter, Instagram, WhatsApp status, or Facebook) or to any third party for entertainment, ridicule, or personal use. Any breach constitutes gross misconduct resulting in **immediate permanent deactivation, reporting to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) for criminal prosecution (carrying statutory fines up to KES 5,000,000 or two years imprisonment), and full contractual indemnity** in favor of the Company.

4.1. **Proof of Lawful Authority:** Where the Driver uses a personally owned vehicle or motorcycle, the Driver shall provide a certified copy of the Logbook. Where the Vehicle is owned by a third-party fleet owner, the Driver shall provide written authorization or a commercial lease agreement from the registered owner.  
4.2. **Approval & Secondary Roadworthiness Layer:** The Vehicle must be approved by the Company before deployment. In addition to statutory NTSA annual inspection stickers, the Vehicle must satisfy secondary roadworthiness confirmation from an accredited automotive service and roadworthiness inspection center.  
4.3. **Motorcycle Standards (Class A2 / A3):** Where the Driver operates a motorcycle:
* The motorcycle must be mechanically sound with functional front and rear brakes, dual rear-view mirrors, working headlights, brake lights, and indicators;
* The Driver must provide two (2) standard-compliant helmets (one for the rider and one for the passenger) and two (2) clean, high-visibility reflective vests/jackets;
* The motorcycle must hold valid commercial motorcycle insurance.  
4.4. **Non-Ownership by Company:** The Driver shall not represent to any party that the Company owns, leases, or holds title to the Vehicle.

### 6. DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE DRIVER
The Driver shall:
6.1. Provide Services with due care, skill, diligence, professionalism, and utmost regard for passenger and public safety.  
6.2. Observe all applicable traffic laws, road speed limits (50 km/h in urban areas / 100 km/h on highways), and traffic control signs.  
6.3. Keep the Vehicle clean, odor-free, mechanically sound, and presentable.  
6.4. Arrive promptly for accepted dispatches and communicate delays via the in-app interface.  
6.5. Treat passengers, Company personnel, and other road users with courtesy, respect, and dignity.  
6.6. Refrain strictly from discrimination, intimidation, harassment, foul language, or abusive behavior.  
6.7. Maintain a distraction-free cabin: mobile phones must be securely mounted on a dashboard cradle. Texting, browsing, or watching videos while driving is strictly prohibited.  
6.8. Never drive while intoxicated or impaired by alcohol, drugs, or fatiguing medication. Zero blood alcohol concentration (0.00% BAC) is enforced.  
6.9. Ensure all passengers buckle up seatbelts before moving (or wear crash helmets on motorcycle trips).  
6.10. Never carry passengers in excess of the legal licensed seating capacity of the Vehicle.  
6.11. Safeguard passenger property and follow the mandatory Lost & Found Protocol.  
6.12. Follow the designated GPS navigation route unless the passenger and driver mutually agree on an alternative route.  
6.13. Never solicit or accept off-platform unbooked trips ("cancel and pay me directly"), which invalidates insurance coverage and platform safety protections.

### 8. FARE COLLECTION, COMMISSION, TAXES AND SETTLEMENTS
8.1. **Driver Responsibility for Fare Collection:**
* On cash-settled and direct payment trips, the Driver is solely responsible for collecting the full calculated trip fare directly from the passenger at the end of the journey before the passenger disembarks.
* On digital trips (mobile money, debit/credit cards, or in-app passenger wallet), the fare is processed through the Cabi Platform payment gateway and credited to the Driver's in-app digital balance.  
8.2. **Statutory Commission Cap (18%):** In compliance with Regulation 9 of the NTSA TNC Regulations, 2022:
* **The Company shall deduct exactly eighteen percent (18%) platform commission from the gross fare of completed trips.**
* **The Driver shall receive eighty-two percent (82%) of the gross trip fare as Net Earnings.**  
* On cash trips where the Driver collects 100% of the fare, the Company debits the statutory 18% commission from the Driver’s digital wallet balance.  
8.3. **Legacy Programme Deductions:**
* **Pension Contribution:** A fixed, mandatory welfare and retirement contribution of **KES 10.00 per completed trip** shall be deducted and remitted directly into the Driver’s individual registered Pension Trust Account under the Cabi Legacy Programme.
* **Welfare & Gamified Savings:** Where the Driver has formally elected to participate in additional Legacy Programme benefits (e.g., medical cover, emergency assistance, or vehicle upgrade fund), a **percentage-based deduction** selected by the Driver shall be deducted from Net Earnings and credited accordingly.  
8.4. **Payout Settlements & Channels:**
* Net earnings accumulated in the Driver’s in-app wallet are withdrawable daily on-demand via **mobile money** (cellular funds transfer) or direct bank transfer to the account registered under the Driver’s verified National ID and KRA PIN.  
8.5. **Driver Tax Obligations & Statutory Compliance:**
* The Driver acknowledges and agrees that they operate as an independent commercial service provider and bear sole legal and statutory responsibility for declaring, filing, and remitting all applicable national and county taxes, levies, or duties (including but not limited to Turnover Tax (TOT) at statutory rates or Individual Income Tax, and municipal parking/pick-up fees) to the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and relevant county governments.
* The Company shall not withhold or deduct income taxes from the Driver’s earnings unless strictly required to do so pursuant to a valid statutory enactment, regulation, or formal written agency notice issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority.
* The Company provides itemized digital earnings statements through the Cabi Driver Application to enable the Driver to fulfill their individual tax filing obligations.

### 10. INSURANCE AND ACCIDENT PROTOCOLS
10.1. The Vehicle must maintain uninterrupted Commercial PSV / Motorcycle Insurance issued by an IRA-registered insurer.  
10.2. In the event of an accident or collision, the Driver shall:
a. Stop immediately and take necessary steps to ensure medical aid and passenger safety;  
b. Contact emergency services and Cabi's 24/7 Emergency Dispatch;  
c. Report the accident to the nearest Police Station within twenty-four (24) hours as required by the Traffic Act;  
d. Obtain insurance particulars of involved third parties;  
e. Refrain from admitting legal liability or promising compensation without Company or insurer authorization.

---

### 11. PASSENGER SAFETY, CUSTOMER SERVICE & ROUTE VARIATION
11.1. The Driver shall prioritize passenger safety and convenience throughout the journey.  
11.2. **Mutual Route Agreement:** The Driver shall navigate using the app's optimal route. However, where the Driver and passenger mutually agree to an alternative or longer route (due to traffic jams, weather, road works, security, or passenger-requested intermediate drop-offs), the actual recorded distance and fare shall be valid and payable without penalty.  
11.3. **In-App SOS Emergency Button:** The Driver and passenger have access to an in-app SOS button. Upon activation, Cabi coordinates emergency intervention with the National Police Service and ambulance providers.

### 12. PROHIBITED CONDUCT & PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES
12.1. The following conduct constitutes a material breach of this Agreement:
a. Reckless or dangerous driving, speeding, or driving while intoxicated;  
b. Assault, violence, intimidation, or sexual harassment of passengers or staff;  
c. Operating without valid driving licenses, PSV badges, inspection stickers, or insurance;  
d. Tampering with platform algorithms, GPS spoofing, or fraudulent trip inflation;  
e. Off-platform trip poaching or coercing passengers into unauthorized cash payments;  
f. Subletting, sharing, or transferring driver login credentials to third parties;  
g. Retaining, misusing, or broadcasting passenger personal contact information or camera footage.  
12.2. **Progressive Disciplinary Penalties (Instead of Arbitrary Deactivation):** To ensure equity and proportional justice, non-criminal operational infractions shall follow a graduated penalty framework:
* **Level 1 (First Warning):** Written digital reprimand and mandatory customer service retraining;
* **Level 2 (Temporary Cooldown):** Dispatch suspension for 24 to 72 hours;
* **Level 3 (Account Restoration Fee & Demotion):** Administrative penalty fee (KES 500 – 1,000) payable via mobile money or wallet deduction, loss of tier incentives, and probation;
* **Level 4 (Permanent Deactivation):** Reserved strictly for repeated non-compliance or severe offenses (violence, DUI, sexual misconduct, criminal fraud, or unauthorized release of in-cabin video).
