### 3. NATURE OF THE RELATIONSHIP, SHIFTS, SAFETY & REFERRALS

* 3.1. **Independent Contractor Arrangement:** The Parties expressly record their relationship as that of an independent service provider. The Driver is engaged as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall create any partnership, joint venture, employer-employee relationship, agency, or master-servant relationship under the Employment Act, 2007.
* 3.2. **Freedom of Operation & Shift Scheduling:** The Driver retains general discretion regarding when to log onto the Cabi Platform. However, in compliance with road safety standards, fatigue prevention rules, and market equilibrium:
  * The Company reserves the right to implement operational shift windows, zone allocation quotas, and active online session limits to manage the number of concurrent online drivers on the network.
  * In adherence to international and national road safety standards, no Driver shall remain on continuous active driving status exceeding twelve (12) consecutive hours without a mandatory rest window of at least six (6) consecutive hours.
* 3.3. **Exclusivity & Multi-Apping:** This Agreement is non-exclusive; the Driver is free to engage in other lawful commercial pursuits and use other software platforms provided they do not compromise active trip safety or violate platform confidentiality.
* 3.4. **Passenger Verification Layer for Driver Safety:** To protect drivers from carjackings, violent crimes, and fraudulent bookings, the Company mandates that all passengers register with a verified, recognizable passport-standard facial photo as their profile avatar and undergo periodic identity authentications (via OTP, selfie capture, or other biometric authentications). The Driver is contractually entitled to visually inspect and confirm that the boarding passenger matches the displayed profile avatar before commencing a trip, and may decline without penalty where there is an identity mismatch.
* 3.5. **Mandatory Onboarding Declaration of In-Cabin Dashcams & Security Systems:**
  * **Affirmative Duty to Declare:** During onboarding, the Driver has an affirmative legal obligation to formally declare to the Company whether the Vehicle is fitted with an in-cabin dashcam or surveillance recording system.
  * **Onboarding Inspection & Decal Proof:** Where an in-cabin camera is installed, the Driver must submit photographic evidence of the camera installation and verify that a clear, visible warning decal is prominently displayed inside the passenger cabin stating:  
    *“Notice: For your safety and security, this vehicle is equipped with an in-cabin camera system. Recordings are encrypted and accessed solely for lawful incident and dispute investigations.”*
  * **Continuous Duty to Disclose:** If a Driver installs, removes, or alters an in-cabin camera after onboarding, the Driver must update their vehicle profile within twenty-four (24) hours. Operating an undeclared surveillance camera constitutes a material breach and grounds for immediate account suspension.
  * **Tamper-Evident Loop Storage:** Recordings must operate on an automated rolling loop overwriting every forty-eight (48) to seventy-two (72) hours, unless an active safety incident or police inquiry is triggered.
  * **Strict Anti-Leak & Social Media Prohibition:** In strict compliance with Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 and constitutional privacy rights, the Driver covenants that they shall **never download, screenshot, share, or upload any passenger audio or video recordings to social media platforms (including TikTok, YouTube, X/Twitter, Instagram, WhatsApp status, or Facebook)** or to any third party for entertainment, ridicule, or personal use. Any breach constitutes gross criminal misconduct resulting in **immediate permanent deactivation, reporting to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) for criminal prosecution (carrying statutory fines up to KES 5,000,000 or two years imprisonment), and full contractual indemnity in favor of the Company.**
* 3.6. **Driver Referral Advocacy & Commuter Onboarding:**
  * **Right to Refer Commuters:** As an independent ambassador, the Driver is fully authorized and encouraged to invite commuters, passengers, and contacts to download and use the Cabi Platform using the Driver’s personal Referral Link or QR Code.
  * **Fair Competition & Customer Choice:** Drivers may introduce Cabi Africa to commuters they encounter in the course of their professional activities (including passengers on non-Cabi or competitor journeys), provided that:
    1. The Driver does not solicit or demand that a passenger cancel an active dispatch mid-trip, which would compromise insurance and passenger safety;
    2. The introduction is conducted respectfully and in compliance with the Competition Act and fair commercial standards;
    3. The Driver earns referral commissions and incentives under the Cabi Referral Programme once the referred passenger registers and completes their qualifying journey on Cabi Africa.

### 4. BIOMETRIC VERIFICATION, ANTI-IMPERSONATION & INVESTIGATIONS

* 4.1. **Driver Identity Biometric Verification:** To ensure passenger trust and combat identity fraud, the Driver agrees to periodic real-time facial biometric scans via the Driver App before logging on or receiving dispatches.
* 4.2. **Strict Purpose Limitation:** All biometric and facial verification templates collected by the Company are encrypted and processed strictly in accordance with Sections 29, 44, and 45 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 for:
  * Verifying that the licensed driver operating the vehicle is the registered account holder;
  * Assisting law enforcement authorities (National Police Service and DCI) in formal criminal investigations into accidents, carjackings, assaults, or robbery;
  * Enforcing anti-recidivism controls to permanently block deregistered or blacklisted offenders from re-enrolling under fraudulent identities.
* 4.3. **No Commercial Exploitation:** Biometric data shall never be commercialized, shared with marketing third parties, or used for unauthorized profiling.

### 5. VEHICLE & MOTORCYCLE ARRANGEMENTS

* 5.1. **Proof of Lawful Authority:** Where the Driver uses a personally owned vehicle or motorcycle, the Driver shall provide a certified copy of the Logbook. Where the Vehicle is owned by a third-party fleet owner, the Driver shall provide written authorization or a commercial lease agreement from the registered owner.
* 5.2. **Approval & Secondary Roadworthiness Layer:** The Vehicle must be approved by the Company before deployment. In addition to statutory NTSA annual inspection stickers, the Vehicle must satisfy secondary roadworthiness confirmation from an accredited automotive service and roadworthiness inspection center.
* 5.3. **Motorcycle Standards (Class A2 / A3):** Where the Driver operates a motorcycle:
  * The motorcycle must be mechanically sound with functional front and rear brakes, dual rear-view mirrors, working headlights, brake lights, and indicators;
  * The Driver must provide two (2) standard-compliant helmets (one for the rider and one for the passenger) and two (2) clean, high-visibility reflective vests/jackets;
  * The motorcycle must hold valid commercial motorcycle insurance.
* 5.4. **Non-Ownership by Company:** The Driver shall not represent to any party that the Company owns, leases, or holds title to the Vehicle.

### 9. FARE COLLECTION, COMMISSION, TAXES AND SETTLEMENTS

* 9.1. **Driver Responsibility for Fare Collection:**
  * On cash-settled and direct payment trips, the Driver is solely responsible for collecting the full calculated trip fare directly from the passenger at the end of the journey before the passenger disembarks.
  * On digital trips (mobile money, debit/credit cards, or in-app passenger wallet), the fare is processed through the Cabi Platform payment gateway and credited to the Driver's in-app digital balance.
* 9.2. **Statutory Commission Cap (18%):** In compliance with Regulation 9 of the NTSA TNC Regulations, 2022:
  * The Company shall deduct exactly **eighteen percent (18%)** platform commission from the gross fare of completed trips.
  * The Driver shall receive **eighty-two percent (82%)** of the gross trip fare as Net Earnings.
  * On cash trips where the Driver collects 100% of the fare, the Company debits the statutory 18% commission from the Driver’s digital wallet balance.
* 9.3. **Legacy Programme Deductions:**
  * **Pension Contribution:** A fixed, mandatory welfare and retirement contribution of **KES 10.00 per completed trip** shall be deducted and remitted directly into the Driver’s individual registered Pension Trust Account under the Cabi Legacy Programme.
  * **Welfare & Gamified Savings:** Where the Driver has formally elected to participate in additional Legacy Programme benefits (e.g., medical cover, emergency assistance, or vehicle upgrade fund), a percentage-based deduction selected by the Driver shall be deducted from Net Earnings and credited accordingly.
* 9.4. **Payout Settlements & Channels:**
  * Net earnings accumulated in the Driver’s in-app wallet are withdrawable daily on-demand via mobile money (cellular funds transfer) or direct bank transfer to the account registered under the Driver’s verified National ID and KRA PIN.
* 9.5. **Driver Tax Obligations & Statutory Compliance:**
  * The Driver acknowledges and agrees that they operate as an independent commercial service provider and bear sole legal and statutory responsibility for declaring, filing, and remitting all applicable national and county taxes, levies, or duties (including but not limited to Turnover Tax (TOT) at statutory rates or Individual Income Tax, and municipal parking/pick-up fees) to the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and relevant county governments.
  * The Company shall not withhold or deduct income taxes from the Driver’s earnings unless strictly required to do so pursuant to a valid statutory enactment, regulation, or formal written agency notice issued by the Kenya Revenue Authority.
  * The Company provides itemized digital earnings statements through the Cabi Driver Application to enable the Driver to fulfill their individual tax filing obligations.

### 13. PROHIBITED CONDUCT & PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINARY PENALTIES

* 13.1. The following conduct constitutes a material breach of this Agreement:
  * a. Reckless or dangerous driving, speeding, or driving while intoxicated;
  * b. Assault, violence, intimidation, or sexual harassment of passengers or staff;
  * c. Operating without valid driving licenses, PSV badges, inspection stickers, or insurance;
  * d. Tampering with platform algorithms, GPS spoofing, or fraudulent trip inflation;
  * e. Off-platform trip poaching or coercing passengers into unauthorized cash payments;
  * f. Subletting, sharing, or transferring driver login credentials to third parties;
  * g. Retaining, misusing, or broadcasting passenger personal contact information or camera footage;
  * h. Engaging in public extortion, orchestrated cyber-harassment, or defamatory smear campaigns against the Company on social media in breach of lawful dispute mechanisms.
* 13.2. **Progressive Disciplinary Penalties (Instead of Arbitrary Deactivation):** To ensure equity and proportional justice, non-criminal operational infractions shall follow a graduated penalty framework:
  * **Level 1 (First Warning):** Written digital reprimand and mandatory customer service retraining;
  * **Level 2 (Temporary Cooldown):** Dispatch suspension for 24 to 72 hours;
  * **Level 3 (Account Restoration Fee & Demotion):** Administrative penalty fee (KES 500 – 1,000) payable via mobile money or wallet deduction, loss of tier incentives, and probation;
  * **Level 4 (Permanent Deactivation):** Reserved strictly for repeated non-compliance or severe offenses (violence, DUI, sexual misconduct, criminal fraud, or unauthorized release of in-cabin video).
