4. SOLIDIFIED OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES & EVIDENTIARY STANDARDS

4.1. The Evidentiary Triangulation Protocol (Natural Justice & Anti-Hearsay)

In strict conformity with the Fair Administrative Action Act (No. 4 of 2015) and Article 47 of the Constitution of Kenya, CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED enforces zero-tolerance for arbitrary suspensions based on uncorroborated allegations.

  1. The Three Evidentiary Pillars: Prior to issuing any permanent account sanction, financial penalty, or deactivation, customer care investigators must triangulate three (3) objective sources of evidence:
  • Pillar 1: GPS Telematics Replay: Precise route traversal, speed logs, timestamps, acceleration/braking telemetry, and geo-fence crossings.
  • Pillar 2: In-Cabin Video/Audio Surveillance: Dashcam recordings where the vehicle is marked with the in-app camera badge and interior decal.
  • Pillar 3: Encrypted Communication Records: In-app masked phone call logs, VoIP timestamps, and in-app chat transcripts.
  1. Right to Be Heard (Audi Alteram Partem): Both the reporting party and the accused party must be afforded a structured opportunity to submit their factual statements and evidence before a final adverse finding is entered.

4.2. Handling Tier 1 Critical Safety Incidents (In-App SOS Trigger)

  1. Instant SOS Telemetry Isolation: Upon SOS button activation:
  • The Security Command Center automatically locks the vehicle's live GPS coordinates;
  • Real-time telemetry speed, driver credentials (photo, PSV badge, phone, number plate), and passenger emergency contact profiles are projected on the emergency dispatch dashboard.
  1. Outbound Emergency Verification: The Security Officer places an immediate outbound call to both parties within three (3) minutes.
  2. Emergency Armed / Medical Dispatch: If physical distress is confirmed or if two call attempts go unanswered:
  • The nearest emergency security/medical response partner is dispatched to the live GPS location;
  • An emergency alert is transmitted to the National Police Service (NPS) 999/112 command desk;
  • The driver’s dispatch capability is placed on an administrative safety freeze pending formal investigation.
  1. Post-Incident Reporting: A comprehensive written Incident Report is compiled and submitted to NTSA and the police within twenty-four (24) hours.

4.3. DCI & Law Enforcement Subpoena Protocol (< 2 Hours for Active Danger)

Where police officers or investigators from the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) request trip data, driver identity, or location logs:

  1. Active Life-Threatening Emergencies (Kidnapping, Armed Robbery): Immediate expedited data sharing within two (2) hours directly with the verified DCI Cybercrime / Special Operations Unit, upon verification of the Officer Commanding Station (OCS) or Investigating Officer (IO).
  2. Routine Criminal Investigations: Full production within twenty-four (24) hours upon receipt of an official Police Letter of Request citing the Occurrence Book (OB) Number.
  3. Statutory Immunity: Compliance with this protocol affords CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED complete statutory immunity from civil privacy actions pursuant to Section 51 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Exemptions for prevention, detection, and prosecution of crime).

4.4. Fast-Track Driver Detailing & Cleaning Fee Disbursement (1-Hour SLA)

To prevent driver partners from suffering extended downtime due to passenger soiling:

  1. Submission: The driver uploads three (3) timestamped photos showing the interior soiling (vomit, spilt grease, pungent liquid mess) along with the vehicle registration plate within twelve (12) hours of trip completion.
  2. Express 1-Hour Verification: Customer care reviews the evidence and immediately approves the fee (KES 1,500 to KES 5,000 according to severity).
  3. Direct Wallet Disbursement: The approved cleaning fee is disbursed directly into the driver partner's wallet from the Cabi Driver Emergency Detailing Pool so the driver can pay for immediate professional detailing.
  4. Passenger Debt Tagging: The approved cleaning fee is simultaneously debited to the passenger's account profile. Future ride requests from that account or device are blocked until the fee is settled.

4.5. M-Pesa Daraja API Direct Reconciliation & Anti-Reversal Enforcement

Where a direct fare settlement dispute occurs (passenger claims to have paid via M-Pesa, driver claims non-receipt, or passenger triggers a fraudulent reversal):

  1. Direct Daraja API Query: Customer Care agents request the official 10-character Safaricom M-Pesa confirmation code (SKL...) and query the Safaricom Daraja API portal to verify:
  • Exact recipient phone / till number;
  • Transaction status (Completed, Failed, Queued); and
  • Exact timestamp of transaction.
  1. Reversal Detection & Sanction: If an active reversal was initiated after passenger egress without lawful justification:
  • The outstanding fare plus a 20% administrative recovery fee is logged as a civil debt on the passenger's profile;
  • The passenger's account is suspended;
  • An automated SMS notification is dispatched citing criminal liability for Obtaining Credit by False Pretenses (Section 316A Penal Code) and Electronic Payment Fraud (Section 35 Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act);
  • If unresolved within forty-eight (48) hours, formal documentation is forwarded to the DCI for criminal tracking.

4.6. Algorithmic Fare Dispute Auditing (Anti-Refund Farming)

  1. The 15% Telematics Route Deviation Threshold: A passenger fare reduction or wallet credit for a "longer route" is granted only if GPS telemetry confirms an unannounced detour exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of the optimal route distance with zero traffic, flooding, or roadblock justification.
  2. Mutually Agreed Detours: Where driver and passenger agreed to detour to avoid traffic or add intermediate stops, the fare remains valid and no deduction is made against the driver.
  3. Anti-Refund Farming Gate: Any passenger account lodging more than two (2) unsubstantiated route overcharge complaints within thirty (30) days is automatically flagged for manual fraud audit, and one-click refund automation is revoked.

4.7. Scripted Response to Social Media Blackmail & Extortion

When an aggressive user attempts to bypass internal resolution by threatening defamatory social media smear campaigns ("Refund me now or I post on Buyer Beware / Twitter / TikTok"):

  1. Mandatory De-escalation Script: Customer Care agents must immediately send the standardized corporate response:

> "CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED is committed to fair, transparent, and evidence-based investigation under our NTSA-approved Dispute Resolution SOP (Ticket #[ID]). Please be formally advised that publishing fabricated, unverified, or extortionate allegations regarding an ongoing investigation constitutes a criminal offense under Section 23 of the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018. Your case has been logged and escalated to Senior Compliance and Legal Officers for expedited determination."

  1. Escalation to Legal: The ticket is transferred to the Legal Officer to monitor social media channels and prepare formal legal demand letters under the Defamation Act (Cap 36) if defamatory falsehoods are published.

4.8. Lost and Found Property Protocol & Courier Compensation

  1. Reporting Window: Passengers must report forgotten items via the app's “Lost Item” tab within thirty (30) calendar days of the trip.
  2. Return Delivery Fee (No Free Courier Obligation):
  • Driver partners are under no obligation to deliver forgotten items across town for free.
  • The passenger may collect the item free of charge from the Cabi Customer Support Office or nearest Police Station; OR
  • Request direct delivery by paying the driver the standard return fare (minimum KES 500) upon delivery.
  1. Chain of Custody for Surrendered Items:
  • Items surrendered at Cabi Offices must be inspected exclusively by the Designated Safety Officer in the presence of an Independent Witness;
  • Two-signatory Lost Property Custody Sheets must record item description, serial numbers, and condition before sealing in tamper-evident security bags;
  • Items are safeguarded in fireproof office safes for up to ninety (90) days pursuant to the Disposal of Uncollected Goods Act (Cap 38).
  • Unclaimed official documents (National IDs, Passports, Bank Cards) are surrendered to the National Registration Bureau / Immigration / Police after 90 days.

Pursuant to Regulation 13 of Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022, CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED shall submit a Quarterly Consolidated Dispute & Safety Report to the NTSA Director of Road Safety containing:

  1. Total customer complaints logged across Tiers 1 to 4;
  2. Average First Response and Resolution Time vs. target SLAs;
  3. Number and nature of road traffic collisions and passenger injuries;
  4. Number of safety deactivations and police OB referrals; and
  5. Audit proof that all driver deductions complied strictly with the 18% commission cap.
