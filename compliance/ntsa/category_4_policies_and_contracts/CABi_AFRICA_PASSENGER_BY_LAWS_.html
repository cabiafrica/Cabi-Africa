3. NATURE AND SCOPE OF THE CABI AFRICA SERVICE

3.1 Platform Services

Cabi Africa operates a digital transportation network that matches registered passengers seeking transportation with independent, vetted drivers offering commercial passenger carriage. Cabi Africa provides software matchmaking, upfront fare estimation, route navigation, digital payment processing, safety tracking, emergency SOS support, and customer care resolution.

3.2 Transportation Services

The actual carriage of passengers is performed by the independent driver assigned to the booking, using a motor vehicle inspected and compliant with NTSA standards. Cabi Africa operates strictly as a Transport Network Company (TNC) under Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022 and does not operate as a direct employer or common carrier, without prejudice to its mandatory statutory obligations under Kenyan transport and consumer protection laws.

3.3 No Guarantee of Instant Availability

Cabi Africa does not guarantee that an available driver will accept every booking request at all hours or in all geographic zones. Estimated times of arrival (ETAs) and journey durations displayed in the app are indicative estimates subject to prevailing traffic conditions, weather, roadblocks, security situations, and mechanical incidents.

3.4 No Guaranteed Exclusive Service

Unless a specialized exclusive or premium service category is selected and confirmed, a booking covers the lawful carriage of the passenger and declared accompanying companions up to the licensed passenger seating capacity of the vehicle. Passengers shall not demand exclusive use beyond the confirmed booking parameters.

3.5 Independent Driver-Sourced Bookings

Where a driver introduces a passenger to the Cabi Africa application, any resulting booking must be conducted strictly through the platform to ensure regulatory compliance, insurance coverage, and passenger safety tracking. Drivers are prohibited from soliciting off-app cash trips, and passengers are advised that off-app journeys are not covered by platform emergency response or insurance.

5. BOOKING PROCEDURE AND ACCEPTANCE OF A RIDE

5.1 Making a Booking

To request a ride, the passenger shall:

a. Select the appropriate vehicle class (Standard Saloon, Comfort, XL, or Boda);

b. Input an accurate pickup location and destination;

c. Review the estimated fare, distance, and route display;

d. Disclose any special luggage or accessibility requirements;

e. Select an approved payment method (Cash, M-Pesa, Card, or Digital Wallet); and

f. Confirm the booking request.

5.2 Booking Confirmation and Driver Verification

A ride is confirmed when a driver accepts the request and the platform displays the driver's full name, profile photo, star rating, vehicle make/model, color, and NTSA registration number plate. The passenger must verify that the vehicle registration plate and driver photo match the app details before boarding.

5.3 Pickup Location and Traffic Compliance

Passengers must select accessible, safe, and legally permissible pickup points. Passengers shall not request drivers to stop in prohibited highway zones, no-parking zones, roundabout lanes, or private properties where entry is unauthorized.

5.4 Changes to Destination or Route

A passenger may request route changes or intermediate stops via the in-app interface. Fares will be automatically recalculated based on the revised distance, duration, and applicable tariff.

5.5 Waiting Time and No-Show Rules

Passengers must be ready at the designated pickup location upon driver arrival. A complimentary grace period of five (5) minutes is provided. Thereafter, standard per-minute waiting charges apply. If a passenger fails to appear within ten (10) minutes without communication, the driver may cancel the trip as a passenger no-show, triggering a disclosed cancellation fee.

5.6 Prohibition on Unauthorised Proxy Bookings

Passengers shall not arrange rides for unknown third parties, fugitives, or individuals engaging in unlawful activities. When booking for a family member or colleague, the account holder remains responsible for ensuring the traveler adheres to these Terms.

6. PASSENGER DOS: REQUIRED STANDARDS OF CONDUCT

Every passenger shall observe the following mandatory standards of conduct:

  • 6.1 Respect and Courtesy: Treat all drivers, support staff, and fellow occupants with dignity, courtesy, and respect. Refrain from abusive, condescending, or aggressive language.
  • 6.2 Identity and Vehicle Verification: Always verify the vehicle registration plate, driver photo, and vehicle color before entering the vehicle.
  • 6.3 Seat Belt Compliance: Fasten and wear safety belts at all times during the journey in compliance with Section 22A of the Traffic Act.
  • 6.4 Respect Road Safety Laws: Never urge, bribe, or instruct a driver to exceed speed limits, run red lights, make illegal U-turns, overlap, or violate traffic regulations.
  • 6.5 Personal Valuables: Keep personal belongings, mobile phones, and luggage secure throughout the trip. Inspect the vehicle interior before alighting.
  • 6.6 Vehicle Cleanliness: Maintain vehicle cleanliness. Do not litter, spill liquids, smoke, vape, or bring foul-smelling items into the vehicle.
  • 6.7 Report Safety Concerns: Promptly report any reckless driving, vehicle mechanical defects, harassment, or irregular behavior through the in-app emergency button or support desk.
  • 6.8 Emergency Cooperation: Cooperate fully with the driver, emergency services, medical personnel, and police in the event of a road traffic collision or emergency.
  • 6.9 Driver Non-Interference: Never touch the steering wheel, handbrake, gear shifter, pedals, or vehicle instruments while the vehicle is in motion.
  • 6.10 Prompt Fare Settlement: Settle the full disclosed fare promptly upon trip completion using an authorized payment method.

7. PASSENGER DON'TS: STRICTLY PROHIBITED CONDUCT

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited and constitute grounds for immediate trip cancellation, account termination, and legal prosecution:

  • 7.1 Abuse, Harassment and Discrimination: Zero tolerance for verbal abuse, racial slurs, tribal discrimination, religious harassment, sexual solicitation, unwanted physical contact, or intimidation.
  • 7.2 Violence and Physical Assault: Striking, threatening, restraining, or assaulting a driver or vehicle occupant is a criminal offense that will result in immediate police referral.
  • 7.3 Fare Evasion and Payment Fraud: Refusing to pay a valid fare, using stolen credit cards, issuing fraudulent M-Pesa reversal claims, or manipulating promotional discounts.
  • 7.4 Prohibited Goods and Contraband: Transporting firearms, ammunition, dangerous weapons, illicit narcotics, uninspected bushmeat, explosive chemicals, or stolen property.
  • 7.5 Severe Intoxication and Disruptive Conduct: Entering the vehicle in an uncontrollable state of alcohol or substance intoxication that creates a safety hazard or compromises vehicle control.
  • 7.6 Vehicle Damage and Soiling: Intentionally or negligently scratching, defacing, damaging upholstery, or vomiting inside the vehicle.
  • 7.7 Identity Spoofing: Using false names, fake avatars, or stolen identity credentials.
  • 7.8 Platform Cyber-Interference: Attempting to reverse engineer, scrape, inject malicious software, or bypass the application’s security protocols.
  • 7.9 Unlawful Recording and Social Media Doxxing: Secretly recording drivers or publishing unauthorized passenger/driver videos on social media (TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, etc.) without lawful authority, in breach of Section 72 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.
  • 7.10 Malicious Ratings and Extortion: Threatening a driver with a 1-star rating or fabricated complaint to extort free rides, discounts, or personal favors.

8. PASSENGER SAFETY, EMERGENCY PROCEDURES AND IN-CABIN CAMERAS

8.1 Safety as an Essential Condition

Passenger and driver safety is paramount. Cabi Africa integrates real-time GPS tracking, 24/7 emergency SOS escalation, and direct linkage to Kenyan law enforcement and emergency medical providers.

8.2 In-Cabin Video and Audio Recording (Dashcams) for On-Trip Safety

To ensure maximum physical security, prevent crime, deter gender-based violence, and resolve serious incident allegations:

  1. Authorization of Safety Cameras: Vehicles operating on the Cabi Africa network may be fitted with inward- and outward-facing dashcams or CCTV security cameras.
  2. Mandatory Notification: Vehicles equipped with in-cabin recording devices must feature visible warning decals informing passengers that video/audio recording is active for safety purposes. The application shall also display a camera indicator upon driver assignment.
  3. Data Protection Act (2019) Compliance: All in-cabin recordings are processed strictly as security surveillance data under Kenyan law. Recordings are encrypted and may only be accessed or extracted by authorized Cabi Africa safety officers for the investigation of reported crimes, traffic accidents, or regulatory audits.
  4. Strict Prohibition on Public Disclosure: Drivers are strictly prohibited from storing, exporting, sharing, or posting camera recordings on social media or private messaging platforms. Any unauthorized leak constitutes a criminal offense and results in immediate permanent blacklisting and prosecution.

8.3 Unsafe Driving and Route Deviation

If a driver operates the vehicle recklessly, exhibits signs of impairment, or deviates drastically from the navigation route without consent, the passenger should request an immediate safe stop and activate the in-app SOS safety trigger.

8.4 Harassment and Threat Response

Passengers experiencing harassment or assault should immediately prioritize their personal safety, exit the vehicle in a well-lit public area when safe to do so, and contact the police and Cabi Africa's 24/7 Safety Command Center at +254 758 777 222.

8.5 Road Traffic Accidents and Medical Emergencies

In the event of a collision, emergency medical services and police must be contacted immediately. Cabi Africa liaises with the vehicle's commercial insurance underwriter to facilitate medical treatment under statutory passenger liability provisions.

8.6 Children and Vulnerable Passengers

Minors under 18 must be accompanied by an adult. Drivers are not childcare providers and cannot assume supervisory custody over unattended children.

9. FARES, PAYMENTS AND DRIVER FARE COLLECTION

9.1 Transparent Fare Calculation

Before confirming a ride, the application displays an upfront fare or clear estimate calculated based on base fare, estimated distance (per kilometer), estimated duration (per minute), applicable tolls, and statutory taxes.

9.2 Driver Fare Collection Responsibility

  1. Direct Collection by Driver: Pursuant to Cabi Africa’s operational model, the driver partner is directly responsible for collecting the trip fare from the passenger upon completion of the trip.
  2. Approved Settlement Channels: Passengers shall settle fares directly with the driver using:
  • Cash (Kenyan Shillings);
  • Direct Mobile Money (M-Pesa payment to the driver's registered Till Number, Paybill, or mobile number as verified on the app); or
  • In-app digital card payment / mobile wallet where enabled.
  1. Receipt Issuance: Upon payment confirmation, the application automatically issues an electronic trip receipt to the passenger’s registered email address and app history.

9.3 Taxes

All fares displayed include applicable Value Added Tax (VAT) and statutory transport charges where required by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). The driver is responsible for their individual income tax and turnover tax compliance.

9.4 Cancellation Fees

A cancellation fee is chargeable if a passenger cancels a ride more than three (3) minutes after driver acceptance or fails to arrive at the pickup location within the designated waiting period. Cancellation fees are waived if the driver was delayed by more than ten (10) minutes beyond the initial ETA.

9.5 Cleaning and Soiling Fees

Where a passenger spills liquids, vomits, or stains vehicle upholstery requiring professional detailing, the passenger shall be liable for reasonable and substantiated cleaning costs (ranging from KES 1,000 to KES 5,000), supported by photographic evidence and commercial cleaning receipts.
