# CABI AFRICA LIMITED
## ACCOUNT DEACTIVATION, SUSPENSION & APPEALS POLICY
### (MANDATORY FAIR HEARING & DUE PROCESS FRAMEWORK)
**COMPLIANCE MANDATE:** REGULATION 14 OF NTSA TNC REGULATIONS, 2022 | ARTICLE 47 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF KENYA (FAIR ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION)

---

## 1. POLICY STATEMENT & GUIDING PRINCIPLES
Cabi Africa Limited is committed to maintaining a safe, reliable, and equitable digital transport ecosystem. In recognition of drivers' livelihoods and constitutional rights under Article 47 of the Constitution of Kenya, Cabi guarantees that:
1. **No driver partner or vehicle owner shall be arbitrarily, summarily, or unfairly deactivated from the Cabi Platform.**
2. All administrative actions shall adhere to the rules of natural justice: notice of allegations, right to be heard, consideration of evidence, and reasoned written decisions.
3. Account suspensions and deactivations shall strictly be enforced based on transparent, pre-published criteria.

---

## 2. GROUNDS FOR ACCOUNT ACTION
Account actions fall under three distinct categories:

### Category A: Immediate Precautionary Suspension (Zero-Tolerance Safety Triggers)
Enacted instantly to prevent imminent danger to the public while an urgent formal investigation is conducted:
* Credible reports of sexual misconduct, harassment, assault, or attempted physical violence;
* Driving under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or psychotropic substances;
* Active criminal offenses, kidnapping, robbery, or weapons possession;
* Lending or subletting verified account credentials to an unregistered third party;
* Unlawful extraction, download, screenshotting, or posting of in-cabin passenger audio or camera recordings to social media or public forums in breach of Section 72 of the Data Protection Act;
* Tampering with vehicle safety equipment or severe vehicle roadworthiness failures (e.g., brake failure).

### Category B: Corrective Warnings, Temporary Hold & Account Reset (Operational Infractions)
Enacted for non-safety operational or quality infractions:
* Consistently poor passenger ratings falling below the regional quality benchmark (4.50 out of 5.00 stars over 100 consecutive trips), following prior retraining warnings;
* Frequent, unprovoked cancellations after trip acceptance exceeding the platform threshold (e.g., > 25% cancellation rate without valid cause);
* Demanding offline cash fares above the in-app fare estimate or refusing to accept digital payments;
* Expiry of statutory documents (Smart DL, NTSA PSV badge, Good Conduct, Inspection, or Insurance).

#### Account Restoration & Reset Mechanism for Category B:
Category B operational holds do **not** trigger permanent deactivation. A driver placed on Category B hold may immediately restore and reset their account status by:
1. **Settlement of Administrative Reset Fee:** Paying a nominal administrative penalty/reinstatement fee (e.g., KES 500 – 1,000) directly via mobile money or in-app wallet balance deduction; OR
2. **Online Refresher Micro-Module:** Completing a 15-minute in-app customer service and etiquette refresher module; OR
3. **Immediate Document Renewal:** Uploading verified, authentic renewed certificates where the hold was triggered by document expiry.
*Once the restoration requirement is met, the system automatically lifts the hold and re-enables active dispatches.*

### Category C: Immediate Permanent Termination
Enacted following conclusive proof of:
* Submission of forged, falsified, or stolen documents (counterfeit Good Conduct, fake NTSA badge, stolen vehicle logbook);
* Conviction in a court of law for a felony, sexual offense, or dangerous driving causing death;
* Severe financial fraud (collusion, GPS spoofing, fraudulent payment reversal scams).

## 3. STEP-BY-STEP DUE PROCESS WORKFLOW

```
  [Trigger / Report / Audit]
              │
              ▼
  [Step 1: Automated Notice of Allegation]
  (Sent within 4 hours; specifies reason, timestamp & evidence summary)
              │
              ▼
  [Step 2: Driver Response & Evidence Submission]
  (Driver has 72 hours to respond via in-app portal or in-person)
              │
              ▼
  [Step 3: Internal Review & Findings]
  (Investigated by Compliance Review Team within 5 business days)
              │
              ▼
    ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
    ▼                                       ▼
[Reinstated / Cleared]           [Action Enacted: Warning, Reset Fee, or Hold]
                                            │
                                            ▼
                                [Step 4: Statutory Right of Appeal]
                                (Within 14 calendar days to Independent Panel)
```

### Detailed Steps:
1. **Written Notice of Specific Allegations:**  
   Within four (4) hours of any suspension, Cabi shall issue a formal written digital notification to the Driver specifying:
   * The exact rule or policy alleged to have been breached;
   * The date, approximate time, and nature of the incident (with passenger personal identifiers masked to protect victim privacy);
   * Whether the account action is a temporary investigatory hold or formal deactivation.
2. **Right to Respond & Submit Evidence:**  
   The Driver shall have seventy-two (72) hours to submit their defense, dashcam footage, GPS telemetry, witness contacts, or written explanation via the Driver App or in person at the Driver Dispute Desk.
3. **Objective Investigation:**  
   The Compliance Review Team examines all evidence—comparing telemetry data, trip speed, audio/video if available, passenger history, and driver record—before rendering a written determination within five (5) working days.
4. **Outcome Notification:**  
   If cleared, the driver’s account is reinstated immediately, and any unwarranted cancellation metrics caused by the review are expunged.

## 4. INDEPENDENT APPEALS COMMITTEE (IAC)
To ensure absolute impartiality, Cabi has constituted the **Cabi Independent Appeals Committee (IAC)**:

### 4.1. Composition of the Committee:
1. **Chairperson:** An external certified dispute mediator or practicing advocate of the High Court of Kenya.
2. **Member:** A representative nominated by registered Driver Welfare Associations or the Cabi Legacy Programme Council.
3. **Member:** Cabi Head of Safety & Operations (non-voting or recused if directly involved in the initial investigation).

### 4.2. Appeals Process:
* A deactivated Driver may lodge an appeal within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of receiving the deactivation decision by completing the online Appeal Request Form, emailing **cabifastafrica@gmail.com**, or submitting a written petition at the Head Office (Ridgeways Commercial Centre, 9 Park Square Mall, F13, Off Kiambu Road, Nairobi).
* The IAC convenes weekly to hear appeals.
* The Driver has the right to attend the hearing (in-person or virtually), be accompanied by a representative or fellow driver, and present additional documentation.
* The IAC renders a final, binding written decision within **seven (7) business days** of the hearing.

---

## 5. REHABILITATION & RETRAINING PROTOCOL
Where a deactivation was caused by low ratings or non-safety customer service complaints, Cabi offers a **Rehabilitation & Retraining Path**:
* The Driver may enroll in an accredited one-day Customer Care & Defensive Driving Refresher Course conducted at the Cabi Training Academy or an approved partner institute.
* Upon passing the post-training assessment and demonstrating mechanical readiness of the vehicle, the Driver’s account is restored on a thirty (30) day probationary tier.

---

## 6. STATUTORY OBLIGATIONS & ARCHIVING
* All deactivation records, written submissions, appeal minutes, and rulings shall be securely retained for a minimum of **five (5) years** and made available for inspection upon statutory demand by NTSA or a court of competent jurisdiction.
