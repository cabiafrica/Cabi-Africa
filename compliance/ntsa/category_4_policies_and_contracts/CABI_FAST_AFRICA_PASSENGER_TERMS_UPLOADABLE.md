## 2. ELIGIBILITY AND PASSENGER PROFILE

1. **Age Requirement:** You must be at least **18 years of age** to register an account and request rides. 
2. **Minors:** Unaccompanied minors under 16 years of age are strictly prohibited from booking or traveling alone. Any trip booked for a minor between 16 and 17 must be authorized by a parent or legal guardian, who assumes full legal and financial responsibility.
3. **Mandatory Passport-Style Photo Avatar:** To safeguard driver and passenger security, every passenger must upload a clear, front-facing, uncovered passport-style photograph during registration. This photo is shared with the assigned driver upon booking confirmation to verify identity before boarding.
4. **Biometric Security & Anti-Recidivism:** Cabi Africa reserves the right to request periodic biometric selfie verifications. Accounts permanently expelled for violent assault, sexual harassment, or fare evasion fraud are securely blacklisted using encrypted biometric hash matching under the Data Protection Act, 2019 to prevent re-registration under alternate SIM cards.

## 3. VEHICLE CAPACITY, LUGGAGE LIMITS AND DISPATCH RULES

1. **Strict Seating Capacity Limits (Section 100 Traffic Act):**
   - **Standard Saloon / Hatchback:** Maximum 4 passengers (one in front, three in rear).
   - **XL / 7-Seater:** Maximum 6 passengers.
   - **Boda (Motorcycle):** Maximum 1 passenger.
   - Squeezing excess passengers into a car or carrying unbelted children on adult laps is a criminal traffic offense under Section 100 of the Traffic Act. Drivers have a mandatory duty to decline overcrowded rides.
2. **Personal Luggage Only (Commercial Cargo Ban):**
   - Vehicles on the Cabi Africa network are licensed exclusively for private passenger carriage and standard personal luggage fitting in the boot.
   - Transporting heavy commercial goods, bulk agricultural produce (sacks of potatoes, grain, etc.), building materials (cement, timber), or greasy spare parts is strictly prohibited. Drivers will refuse commercial cargo trips.
3. **The Driver is the Master of the Vehicle:**
   - The driver partner exercises exclusive authority over speed, route safety, and lawful stopping zones.
   - Passengers are strictly barred from demanding, pressuring, or bribing drivers to overlap, drive on sidewalks, beat red lights, exceed speed limits, or stop in illegal yellow curb / County Council (*Kanjo*) clamping zones.
   - Any fine, wheel-clamping penalty, or towing charge directly resulting from a passenger's unlawful stopping demand shall be fully indemnified and billed to the passenger.
4. **Waiting Times:** A 5-minute complimentary grace period applies upon driver arrival. Thereafter, standard per-minute waiting fees accrue. After 10 minutes of passenger unresponsiveness, the driver may cancel the trip as a "No-Show".

## 4. PASSENGER CODE OF CONDUCT: DOS AND DON'TS

### MANDATORY DOs:
* **DO** verify the driver's face, vehicle model, and registration plate before opening the door or boarding.
* **DO** buckle your seatbelt at all times in compliance with Section 22A of the Traffic Act.
* **DO** treat your driver partner with dignity, courtesy, and respect.
* **DO** pay the agreed trip fare directly to the driver partner via cash or verified M-Pesa immediately upon arrival.
* **DO** check the vehicle back seat and floor for your phone, keys, and personal belongings before exiting.
* **DO** use the in-app SOS emergency button if you experience a genuine safety or physical emergency.

### STRICT PROHIBITIONS (DON'Ts):
* **NO EATING OR DRINKING IN THE VEHICLE:** Consuming fast food, greasy snacks (chips, fried chicken, sausages, samosas), open coffee/tea, or sticky drinks inside the vehicle without prior driver permission is strictly prohibited. Only sealed, screw-capped bottled water is allowed.
* **NO ALCOHOL, SMOKING, VAPING OR MIRAA:** Consuming alcohol inside the car violates the Alcoholic Drinks Control Act. Smoking tobacco, using e-cigarettes/vapes (Tobacco Control Act), or chewing *miraa/muguka* is strictly banned. Drivers have the statutory right to terminate trips immediately with zero refund.
* **NO ABUSE OR SEXUAL HARASSMENT:** Zero tolerance for verbal insults, sexual solicitation, indecent exposure, or unwanted physical contact.
* **NO CONTRABAND OR WEAPONS:** Firearms, dangerous weapons, illicit narcotics, uninspected game meat, and stolen property are prohibited. Drivers will drive directly to the nearest police station.
* **NO INTERFERENCE WITH VEHICLE CONTROLS:** Never touch the steering wheel, handbrake, gear shifter, or pedals.
* **NO PETS WITHOUT PRIOR CONSENT:** Farm livestock/poultry are strictly banned. Domestic pets are only allowed in clean, leak-proof pet carriers with the driver's prior consent (certified service animals for visually impaired commuters are always permitted).

3. **Informed Consent:** By confirming your ride and boarding a vehicle marked with the camera badge, you give your express, informed consent to video/audio recording solely for safety and security purposes under Sections 28–30 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Drivers are strictly prohibited from exporting, saving, or leaking passenger footage to social media. Any leak is a criminal offense under Section 72 of the DPA.

1. **Upfront Fare:** The app displays transparent upfront fares calculated from base fare, distance, duration, tolls, and statutory taxes.
2. **Direct Settlement to Driver:** Driver partners are independent contractors authorized to collect the full fare directly upon trip completion via:
   - Cash (exact Kenya Shillings);
   - Driver's verified M-Pesa Buy Goods Till, Paybill, or registered mobile number; or
   - In-app digital wallet where available.
3. **Strict Criminal Liability for Fare Evasion & M-Pesa Reversals:**
   - Evading a fare, refusing to pay, escaping from a vehicle, or executing a fraudulent, bad-faith M-Pesa reversal constitutes **Obtaining Credit by False Pretenses (Section 316A Penal Code)** and **Electronic Payment Fraud (Section 35 Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act)**.
   - Any unpaid fare is flagged as an active civil debt, blocks future bookings on the passenger's phone/device, and will be reported to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) for arrest and prosecution.

## 7. CLEANING AND INTERIOR SOILING FEES

Passengers are legally liable for maintaining vehicle cleanliness. Where a passenger spills liquids, food grease, vomits, or soils the upholstery:
* **Minor Spills / Food Crumbs:** Vacuuming & stain removal fee: **KES 1,000 to KES 1,500**.
* **Major Food Grease / Sauces / Animal Mess:** Chemical shampoo detailing: **KES 2,000 to KES 3,000**.
* **Vomit / Bodily Fluids / Odor Decontamination:** Full interior foam extraction, ozone treatment, and driver downtime compensation: **KES 3,500 to KES 5,000**.
Fees are verified via photographic proof and billed directly to the passenger's account profile before subsequent ride dispatches.
