### STRATEGIC ADVOCACY & COMPETITIVE BENCHMARKING (UBER, BOLT, LITTLE)
* **Strict Statutory Commission Compliance:** Where competitors faced High Court litigation over predatory commissions, Cabi hardcodes the **18% statutory cap** directly into backend services and contractual terms.
* **Independent Due Process:** Cabi’s **Independent Appeals Committee (IAC)** provides institutional due process, ring-fences driver wallet balances within seven (7) days, and decouples innocent vehicle owners from individual driver infractions.
* **Mutual Safety & Cabin Standards:** Cabi introduces the first comprehensive passenger standards in Kenyan ride-hailing: mandatory profile avatars, biometric checks, absolute eating/intoxicant bans, soiling indemnities, and ODPC-certified in-cabin safety dashcams with strict criminal sanctions against social media leaks.
* **Fair Market Referrals:** Legally protected driver and rider referral incentives under Section 21 of the Competition Act, fostering organic market expansion without in-transit contract interference.
