### MINUTE 02/SPECIAL/2026: APPLICATION FOR NTSA TRANSPORT NETWORK COMPANY (TNC) OPERATOR LICENSE
The Managing Director presented a detailed briefing on the operational deployment of the **Cabi Africa** digital ride-hailing ecosystem comprising the Cabi Passenger Application, Cabi Driver Application, Dispatch Platform, and Core Administrative Infrastructure.

The Board noted that under the **National Transport and Safety Authority Act (No. 33 of 2012)** read together with the **National Transport and Safety Authority (Transport Network Companies, Owners, Drivers and Passengers) Regulations, 2022**, any entity providing digital ride-hailing aggregation services within the Republic of Kenya must hold a valid Transport Network Company (TNC) License.

Following detailed deliberations on technical readiness, safety architectures, regulatory compliance, and statutory capital requirements, it was **UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED**:

> **RESOLUTION 1:**  
> **"THAT** Cabi Africa Limited formally apply to the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) for a Transport Network Company (TNC) Operator License in accordance with the Transport Network Companies Regulations, 2022."

> **RESOLUTION 2:**  
> **"THAT** the statutory license fee of Kenya Shillings One Hundred Thousand (KES 100,000) payable to the Authority through the official eCitizen/NTSA payment gateway be and is hereby approved and allocated for disbursement."

---

### MINUTE 03/SPECIAL/2026: APPOINTMENT OF AUTHORIZED SIGNATORIES AND REGULATORY LIAISONS
To facilitate seamless engagement with the Authority, BRS, KRA, and ODPC, it was **UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED**:

> **RESOLUTION 3:**  
> **"THAT** the Managing Director and Chief Technology Officer be and are hereby jointly and severally authorized as the Designated Official Representatives and Authorized Signatories of Cabi Africa Limited to:
> 1. Sign, execute, seal, and deliver all formal applications, statutory declarations, affidavits, compliance undertakings, and documents required by NTSA.
> 2. Represent the Company before the NTSA Transport Safety Directorate, Licensing Committee, and County Regulatory Offices.
> 3. Manage the Company’s official eCitizen organization portal and lodge all digital hailing submissions on behalf of the Board."

---

### MINUTE 04/SPECIAL/2026: ADOPTION OF STATUTORY COMMISSION CAP AND OPERATIONAL POLICIES
The Board reviewed the technical specifications of Cabi Africa's core payment and dispatch architecture. It was noted that Kenyan law strictly caps platform commissions at eighteen percent (18%). It was **UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED**:

> **RESOLUTION 4:**  
> **"THAT** the Board formally ratify and enforce that Cabi Africa Limited’s platform commission rate shall not exceed eighteen percent (18%) of the total gross fare per completed trip, in strict adherence to Regulation 9 of the NTSA TNC Regulations, 2022."

> **RESOLUTION 5:**  
> **"THAT** the accompanying governance documentation, specifically:
> * The Cabi Driver Services Agreement (drawn by Mwangi Mungai Law Advocates);
> * The Cabi Vehicle Owner Services Agreement (drawn by Mwangi Mungai Law Advocates);
> * The Passenger Terms and Conditions of Use & Ride-Hailing Policy;
> * The Complaints & Dispute Resolution Standard Operating Procedure;
> * The Account Deactivation & Appeals Due Process Policy; and
> * The Community Safety Code of Conduct,
> be and are hereby officially approved and adopted as the mandatory binding operating policies of Cabi Africa Limited."
