MINUTE 01/SPECIAL/2026: CONSTITUTION OF MEETING

The Chairperson took the chair and declared the meeting duly constituted in accordance with the Articles of Association of the Company and the Companies Act, 2015, a quorum of directors being present throughout the deliberations.

MINUTE 02/SPECIAL/2026: APPLICATION FOR NTSA TRANSPORT NETWORK COMPANY (TNC) OPERATOR LICENSE

The Managing Director presented a comprehensive briefing on the operational deployment of the Cabi Africa digital ride-hailing ecosystem comprising the Cabi Passenger Mobile Application, Cabi Driver Partner Mobile Application, Vehicle Owner Fleet Portal, Real-Time Dispatch and Telematics Engine, and Core Safety Infrastructure.

The Board noted that under the National Transport and Safety Authority Act (No. 33 of 2012) read together with the National Transport and Safety Authority (Operation of Transport Network Companies) Regulations, Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022, any corporate entity providing digital hailing aggregation and dispatch services within the Republic of Kenya must hold a valid Transport Network Company (TNC) License issued by the Authority.

Following detailed deliberations on technical readiness, data protection safeguards under the Data Protection Act (2019), vehicle safety requirements, and statutory capital obligations, it was UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED:

> RESOLUTION 1:

> "THAT CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED formally apply to the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) for a Transport Network Company (TNC) Operator License in accordance with the Transport Network Companies Regulations, Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022."

> RESOLUTION 2:

> "THAT the statutory license fee of Kenya Shillings One Hundred Thousand (KES 100,000) payable to the Authority through the official eCitizen/NTSA payment gateway be and is hereby approved and authorized for immediate disbursement."

MINUTE 03/SPECIAL/2026: APPOINTMENT OF AUTHORIZED SIGNATORIES AND REGULATORY LIAISONS

To facilitate seamless engagement with the Authority, Business Registration Service (BRS), Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), it was UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED:

> RESOLUTION 3:

> "THAT the Managing Director and Chief Technology Officer be and are hereby jointly and severally authorized as the Designated Official Representatives and Authorized Signatories of CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED to:

> 1. Sign, execute, seal, and deliver all formal applications, statutory declarations, affidavits, compliance undertakings, and documentation required by NTSA.

> 2. Represent the Company before the NTSA Transport Safety Directorate, Licensing Committee, and County Regulatory Offices.

> 3. Manage the Company’s official eCitizen organization portal and lodge all digital hailing submissions on behalf of the Board."

MINUTE 04/SPECIAL/2026: ADOPTION OF STATUTORY COMMISSION CAP AND OPERATIONAL POLICIES

The Board reviewed the technical specifications of Cabi Africa's core payment and dispatch architecture. It was noted that Kenyan law strictly caps platform commissions at eighteen percent (18%). It was UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED:

> RESOLUTION 4:

> "THAT the Board formally ratify and enforce that CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED’s platform commission rate shall not exceed eighteen percent (18%) of the total gross fare per completed trip, in strict adherence to Regulation 9 of the NTSA TNC Regulations, Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022."

> RESOLUTION 5:

> "THAT the accompanying governance documentation, specifically:

> * The Cabi Fast Africa Driver Partner Services Agreement (drawn by Mwangi Mungai Law Advocates);

> * The Cabi Fast Africa Vehicle Owner Services Agreement (drawn by Mwangi Mungai Law Advocates);

> * The Cabi Fast Africa Passenger Terms and Conditions of Use, Ride-Hailing Policy, and Passenger Code of Conduct;

> * The Customer Care, Incident Management and Dispute Resolution Standard Operating Procedure (SOP);

> * The Account Deactivation, Disciplinary and Appeals Due Process Policy;

> * The Community Safety Code of Conduct and Safety Charter; and

> * The Driver and Vehicle Onboarding Operational Guidelines,

> be and are hereby officially approved, ratified, and adopted as the mandatory binding operating statutes of CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED."

MINUTE 05/SPECIAL/2026: ADJOURNMENT

There being no other business, the Chairperson declared the meeting closed at 12:30 PM.
