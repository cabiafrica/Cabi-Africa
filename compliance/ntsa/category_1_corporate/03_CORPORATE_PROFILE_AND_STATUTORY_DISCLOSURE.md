## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED** is a Kenyan-incorporated mobility technology enterprise established to deliver safe, transparent, dependable, and socially responsible digital passenger transportation network services across the Republic of Kenya. Cabi operates through an integrated digital hailing ecosystem connecting verified commercial Public Service Vehicle (PSV) driver partners, licensed vehicle fleet owners, and commuting passengers via proprietary mobile applications, cloud telematics dispatch engines, and specialized administrative management consoles.

This corporate profile and governance disclosure statement is formally submitted in compliance with the **National Transport and Safety Authority Act (No. 33 of 2012)** and the **National Transport and Safety Authority (Operation of Transport Network Companies) Regulations, Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022**.

## 4. TECHNICAL PLATFORM & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
The Cabi Africa platform operates on an enterprise-grade, microservices cloud infrastructure engineered to meet and exceed NTSA technical and safety standards:

1. **Cabi Passenger Application (Android & iOS):**  
   - Allows passengers to book rides, view transparent upfront fare calculations, inspect driver credentials, and verify vehicle registration details prior to boarding.
   - **In-Cabin Camera Notice Badge:** Automatically alerts passengers whenever an assigned vehicle is fitted with an active safety dashcam, ensuring informed consent under the Data Protection Act, 2019.
   - **Mandatory Photo Avatar & Biometrics:** Features mandatory passport-style profile photos and biometric verification challenges to eliminate fake accounts and impersonation.
   - **Real-Time GPS Tracking & Red SOS Trigger:** Connects directly with the Cabi 24/7 Security Command Center and local emergency services.

2. **Cabi Driver Partner Application (Android & iOS):**  
   - Empowers screened PSV-licensed drivers (Class D1 for saloon/cabs; Class A2/A3 for boda motorcycles) to receive ride dispatches, access turn-by-turn navigation, and view net earnings.
   - **Affirmative Dashcam Declaration:** Drivers declare during onboarding whether their vehicle has an active surveillance camera and verify proper placement of passenger notice decals.
   - **Direct Fare Collection:** Supports transparent direct passenger fare settlement via Cash or verified M-Pesa.
   - **Driver Welfare & Pension Scheme:** Integrates voluntary KES 10/trip welfare and pension deductions to support driver financial security.

3. **Cabi Vehicle Owner Fleet Portal (Web / Mobile):**  
   - Provides a 24/7 read-only telematics dashboard for vehicle owners, institutional fleet managers, and diaspora investors.
   - Enables real-time tracking of vehicle mileage, driver shift performance, maintenance schedules, and geofencing.

4. **Telematics Dispatch Engine & 24/7 Security Command Center:**  
   - Automated intelligent dispatch, speed telemetry, cross-border geofencing alerts, route replay logging, and instant driver deactivation capabilities.

## 5. STATUTORY UNDERTAKINGS TO THE NATIONAL TRANSPORT AND SAFETY AUTHORITY
CABI FAST AFRICA LIMITED hereby solemnly provides the following binding statutory undertakings to the Authority:

1. **Statutory 18% Commission Cap:** The Company strictly adheres to Regulation 9 of Legal Notice No. 120 of 2022 and covenants that it will never deduct, charge, or withhold platform commission exceeding eighteen percent (18%) of the gross fare per completed trip.
2. **Mandatory Driver & Vehicle Vetting:** Cabi Africa will never onboard, activate, or dispatch rides to:
   - Any driver lacking an authentic NTSA PSV Badge, valid Smart Driving License (Class D1/A2), and current DCI Certificate of Good Conduct; or
   - Any vehicle lacking an annual NTSA Roadworthiness Inspection Certificate and comprehensive Commercial PSV Chauffeur-Driven Insurance.
3. **Law Enforcement & Incident Cooperation:** Cabi Africa maintains a 24/7 Safety Command Center that shall promptly cooperate with the National Police Service (NPS), DCI, and NTSA in investigating any road traffic collision, passenger grievance, or security incident.
4. **Data Protection & ODPC Compliance:** All personal data, GPS telematics, and in-cabin surveillance recordings are processed and encrypted in strict conformity with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 under ODPC supervision.
