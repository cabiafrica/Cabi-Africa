## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
**Cabi Africa Limited** is a Kenyan-incorporated technology and mobility enterprise founded to provide safe, dependable, transparent, and technology-driven digital transportation aggregation services across the Republic of Kenya. Cabi operates through an integrated ecosystem connecting verified commercial Public Service Vehicle (PSV) drivers, licensed fleet owners, and commuting passengers via proprietary smartphone mobile applications and dispatch architectures.

This corporate disclosure is submitted in compliance with the **National Transport and Safety Authority Act (No. 33 of 2012)** and the **National Transport and Safety Authority (Transport Network Companies, Owners, Drivers and Passengers) Regulations, 2022**.

## 4. TECHNICAL PLATFORM & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
The Cabi Africa platform operates on a robust, microservices-driven cloud architecture engineered to meet and exceed NTSA standards:

1. **Cabi Passenger App (Android & iOS):**  
   Enables riders to book rides, view transparent upfront fare estimates (Base + Distance + Time + 18% Fee), verify driver and vehicle credentials in real time, monitor trips via live GPS, trigger instant in-app SOS emergency dispatch, and rate service quality.
2. **Cabi Driver App (Android & iOS):**  
   Empowers screened PSV-badged drivers (Class D1 for cabs / Class A2/A3 for bikes) to accept dispatches, access turn-by-turn navigation, trigger driver emergency alarms, track transparent net earnings (82%), and manage shifts and Legacy Programme savings.
3. **Dispatch & Safety Engine:**  
   Automates intelligent driver allocation, geofencing, route logging, speed telemetry, and automated audit trails for regulatory compliance.
4. **Administrative & Dispatch Console:**  
   Centralized management panel equipped with real-time fleet surveillance, dispute tracking ticketing, driver document validity alarms, and instant deactivation capabilities.

## 5. STATUTORY UNDERTAKINGS TO NTSA
Cabi Africa Limited hereby provides formal statutory undertakings to the National Transport and Safety Authority:

1. **18% Commission Cap:** The platform guarantees that it will never charge, deduct, or withhold more than eighteen percent (18%) platform commission from any driver on any trip.
2. **Mandatory Driver & Vehicle Vetting:** Cabi Africa will never onboard, activate, or dispatch rides to any driver without an authentic NTSA PSV Badge, valid Smart DL (Class D1/A2), and current DCI Good Conduct Certificate, nor any vehicle lacking a valid NTSA Roadworthiness Inspection Certificate and Commercial PSV Insurance.
3. **Emergency Incident Cooperation:** Cabi Africa maintains a 24/7 Safety Response Team that shall promptly cooperate with the National Police Service (NPS) and NTSA in investigating any safety, criminal, or traffic incidents.
4. **Data Privacy & Protection:** All trip, location, and user data collected is governed by the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, under ODPC oversight.
