DRIVER & VEHICLE ONBOARDING OPERATIONAL GUIDE

Standard Operating Procedure for Statutory Screening, Forensic Vetting & Continuous Regulatory Audits
Regulatory Basis: NTSA (TNC) Regulations, 2022 | Traffic Act (Cap 403) | Data Protection Act, 2019 | Competition Act (Cap 504)

1. THE REGULATORY MANDATE

Under Regulations 6, 7, and 8 of the National Transport and Safety Authority (Transport Network Companies, Owners, Drivers and Passengers) Regulations, 2022, a licensed TNC is strictly prohibited from dispatching rides to any driver or motor vehicle that has not undergone prior independent statutory verification.

Allowing an unvetted driver or an uninspected vehicle onto the Cabi dispatch engine constitutes a strict liability offense under Kenyan law, exposing the company to:

  1. Immediate regulatory suspension or revocation of the TNC Operator License;
  2. Corporate penalties and personal criminal prosecution of company directors;
  3. Complete repudiation of insurance claims by underwriters in the event of an accident.

GATE 1: Identity & Age Qualification

  • Statutory Requirement: Must be a Kenyan citizen (or valid work-permit holder) aged at least 24 years of age (Traffic Act statutory minimum for commercial passenger transport).
  • Document Required: Original Kenyan National Identity Card (front & back) or valid Kenyan Passport.
  • Verification Steps:
    1. Inspect the date of birth on the ID card; ensure the applicant has attained at least 24 years.
    2. Cross-check the name on the ID card against the KRA PIN certificate and registered mobile money number (must match exactly).
    3. Require a live "liveness selfie" during app registration to prevent identity theft.

GATE 2: Smart Driving License (Smart DL) Verification

  • Statutory Requirement: A valid, unexpired NTSA Smart Driving License card with the appropriate professional vehicle class endorsement.
  • Acceptable Class Endorsements:
    • Class D1 (Professional): Light passenger vehicles / taxi cabs (up to 7 passengers).
    • Class A2 / A3: Commercial motorcycles (boda boda / delivery / courier).
    • (Note: Class B1 Private is NOT legally sufficient for commercial ride-hailing).
  • Validation: Scan the NTSA QR code on the back of the Smart DL using the TIMS verification system to confirm authenticity, driver identity, and active professional class.

GATE 3: DCI Police Clearance & Secondary Trust Vetting

  • Statutory Requirement: A valid Police Clearance Certificate issued by the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) issued within the last six (6) months.
  • Digital Verification Steps:
    1. Locate the official DCI QR code on the top-right corner of the certificate.
    2. Scan the QR code to verify it resolves to an official Government of Kenya URL (https://dci.ecitizen.go.ke/...).
    3. Confirm full legal name, ID number, issue date, and criminal record status (must state "NEGATIVE").
    4. Reject and blacklist any application presenting forged, altered, or expired certificates.

GATE 4: NTSA PSV Driver Badge & Welfare Affiliation

  • Statutory Requirement: A valid NTSA Public Service Vehicle (PSV) Badge endorsing the driver for digital hailing operations.
  • Verification: Verify the eCitizen NTSA PSV Badge Verification Slip; confirm category specifies "PSV - Taxi / Digital Hailing", and confirm registration in the Cabi Legacy Programme or an accredited transport welfare association.

GATE 5: Driver Induction, Cleanliness & Ethics Training

  • Mandatory 2-Hour Driver Induction Course:
    1. Platform Operations: App navigation, fare collection, in-app wallet, and dual-referral buttons (Invite Driver and Invite Rider).
    2. Emergency SOS: Operating the in-app SOS emergency broadcast button and contacting the 24/7 hotline (0758 777 222).
    3. Customer Dignity & Cleanliness: Zero tolerance for harassment, handling disabled passengers, and enforcing the ban on eating, drinking open beverages (only sealed water bottles), alcohol, smoking, vaping, and miraa. Fast-track photo protocol for claiming KES 1,500 – KES 3,500 detailing fees.
    4. Marketing & Competition Ethics: Promoting Cabi to commuters under Section 21 of the Competition Act, with strict prohibition against soliciting in-transit competitor trip cancellations.

3. VEHICLE VERIFICATION & ROADWORTHINESS AUDIT

1. Proof of Ownership (Logbook Verification)

  • Requirement: Official NTSA Logbook (Form E). If the driver is not the registered owner, a signed Vehicle Lease Agreement / Power of Attorney authorizing commercial ride-hailing must be provided.
  • Vehicle Age: Maximum 8 to 10 years from the year of manufacture.

2. NTSA Motor Vehicle Inspection Certificate

  • Requirement: Annual Roadworthiness Inspection conducted at an official NTSA Inspection Center. The windscreen sticker must show at least 30 days of validity remaining and vehicle class registered as "Commercial / PSV".

3. Commercial PSV / Digital Hailing Insurance

  • Requirement: Comprehensive Commercial PSV (Digital Hailing / Chauffeur-Driven) Insurance policy covering Passenger Legal Liability (PLL), Third-Party Property Damage, and Driver Personal Accident. Standard Private Comprehensive insurance is strictly rejected.

4. In-Cabin Dashcam Screening & ODPC Privacy Gateway

  • Declaration: Driver marks hasDashcam: true in the Driver App.
  • Decal Verification: Driver uploads a clear photograph verifying the passenger warning decal is prominently displayed in the cabin.
  • Statutory Undertaking: Driver signs the digital undertaking acknowledging Section 72 of the Data Protection Act (criminal liability for leaking footage on social media).
  • Approval: Compliance officer approves dashcamDecalVerified: true, activating the in-app camera badge.

5. Multi-Point Mechanical Safety Audit

  • Audit includes braking systems, tires (min 3mm tread + spare), seatbelts on all seats, crack-free windscreens, and safety kit (1kg/2kg dry-powder fire extinguisher + 2 reflective lifesavers + first aid kit).
