## 1. Stories

### 1.1. Driver: Referrals for Rewards
**As a** driver,
**I want to** refer new drivers to the platform and earn a commission from their registration fee,
**So that** I am financially rewarded for helping to grow the driver network.

### 1.2. Admin: Configure MLM Rules
**As an** administrator,
**I want to** configure the fee distribution formula for the MLM referral program,
**So that** I have control over the business logic for referral payouts.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1:** A unique referral code/link is automatically generated for every active driver.
- **AC2:** Drivers can access and easily share their referral code/link from within the driver app.
- **AC3:** When a new prospect signs up using a referral code, the system correctly attributes the referral.
- **AC4:** The registration fee is collected upon a new driver's successful registration.
- **AC5:** The system automatically distributes the registration fee according to the admin-configured formula (e.g., portions to the system, direct referrer, and second-level referrer).
- **AC6:** An admin interface exists to create, view, and update the MLM fee distribution rules.
- **AC7:** Drivers can view a history of their successful referrals and the earnings generated from them.
- **AC8:** The referral relationship (parent, child) is stored and auditable.
