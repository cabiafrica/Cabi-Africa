---

## 1. Stories

### 1.1. Driver: Join or Create a Union
**As a** driver,
**I want to** either create a new driver union or join an existing one,
**So that** I can organize with fellow drivers for representation and community.

### 1.2. Union Leader: Manage Union Structure
**As a** union leader,
**I want to** manage my union's membership and create internal subgroups,
**So that** I can structure the union effectively and delegate responsibilities.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1:** Drivers have an option in their profile to either "Create a New Union" or "Find a Union".
- **AC2:** Creating a union requires a unique name and designates the creator as the initial union leader/admin.
- **AC3:** Drivers can search, filter, and browse a directory of existing unions.
- **AC4:** Drivers can submit a request to join a union, which is sent to the union's leader(s) for approval.
- **AC5:** Union leaders have a dashboard to approve or deny membership requests.
- **AC6:** Union leaders can create and name subgroups within their union.
- **AC7:** Union leaders can assign members of their union to one or more subgroups.
- **AC8:** Drivers can view their union membership status and any subgroups they belong to.
