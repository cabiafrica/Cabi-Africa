# Epic: Corporate Accounts

**Status:** Planned
**Priority:** High

## Description
Enable B2B partnerships by allowing companies to create corporate accounts. Employees can link their profiles to the company account to charge rides directly to the company, while corporate admins can manage employees and view spending reports.

## Goals
1. Open a new revenue stream via B2B contracts.
2. Simplify expense reporting for business travelers.
3. Provide companies with control over ride spending.

## Scope
- Admin Panel: Corporate Registration, Dashboard, Employee Management.
- Passenger App: Corporate Payment Method selection.
- Backend: Corporate Service, Billing/Invoicing.

## Key Stories
- Corporate Account Registration
- Corporate Dashboard
- Employee Management
- Corporate Payment Method
